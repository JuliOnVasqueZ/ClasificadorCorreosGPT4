Because the soft cap is applied to the PX, but not to other scheduling coordinators, it will drive suppliers away from the PX. Allowing more forward contracting of net suppliers will reduce market power, but it will be very expensive without an effective bid cap. To be effective, a bid cap must allow no exceptions and must also be a regional cap to avoid causing reliability problems for California.
