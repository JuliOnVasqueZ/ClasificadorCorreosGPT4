19Nov2001 RSF-Enron sounds debt alarm, reduces reported earnings By C. Bryson Hull HOUSTON, Nov 19 (Reuters) - Humbled energy giant Enron Corp. The Houston company sounded the debt and credit alarms once again in a filing made on Monday with U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission. Among the new information included is the disclosure that Enron is up against a short deadline to meet a $690 million debt obligation triggered by a credit downgrade last week.
