The bond markets reacted strongly to comments from U.S. Treasury Secretary Lawrence Summers, who said the U.S. government would buy back securities on all parts of the yield curve (both short-term and long-term), not just the 30-year bond, to pay down government debt. The statements caused long bond rates to jump considerably, which contributed to a sell off in stocks.
