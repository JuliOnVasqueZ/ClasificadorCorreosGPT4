For example, any downgrade, not just a downgrade resulting in a sub-investment grade rating, may entitle a counterparty to require Enron to provide additional credit in support of Enron's purchase obligations. Counterparties should examine all power, gas or other commodity purchase and sale documentation to confirm whether they possess this right. The failure to exercise this right may constitute a waiver and foreclose access to critical performance and payment assurance, threatening to alter the risk assessment and mitigation considerations upon which the transactions were originally based.
