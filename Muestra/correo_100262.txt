The signing of AB 970 established the Governor's Clean Energy Green Team, which works to streamline the process of creating new power plants to ensure that an adequate supply of power will exist to make a deregulated marketplace work. The Team does this by working with local governments, identifying environmental impacts, developing recommendations for low interest financing programs for renewable energy and obtaining input on natural gas supply, emission offsets and water supply. Members do not receive a salary. This position does not require Senate confirmation. Julee Malinowski-Ball Edson
