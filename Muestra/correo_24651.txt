"Whenever you turn a pollution cut into a financial asset," says Joseph Goffman, an attorney at Environmental Defense, "people go out and make lots of pollution cuts." Even where green fervor is of a paler shade, corporations are viewing the potential for global regulation as a business risk they need to consider.
