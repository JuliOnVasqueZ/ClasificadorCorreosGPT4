PCB-containing lubricating oils once were used routinely by a number of U.S. pipeline companies in their gas pipeline compressors, which help push the fuel through the pipes. While they are no longer used, some of the oils have remained inside some pipelines since the 1960s and 70s. If present, they are carried in liquid droplets in the natural gas stream.
