Terrorism puts sand in the gears of cross-border connectivity and the result threatens the increasingly frictionless world of globalisation. The tragic events of September 11 have, in effect, levied a new tax on such flows. The security of national borders will now have to be tightened - hardly a costless endeavour. That will affect more than airports and shipping ports. The porous borders of Canada and Mexico, the conduits of increasingly seamless Nafta linkages, will be tightened too.
