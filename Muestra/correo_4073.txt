Preferred Skills
Special Characteristics

ascertain the financial health and viability of Enron's counterparties.
The candidate must be able to adapt and work effectively with groups which
have diverse interests.
The role requires precision and good organizational skills with an emphasis
for meeting deadlines and prioritizing.
The candidate must be able to excel in an atmosphere of change, adapting to
the constantly changing needs of the credit group.
