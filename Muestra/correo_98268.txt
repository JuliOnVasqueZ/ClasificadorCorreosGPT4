The study finds that the chief determinants of satisfaction among electric utility midsize customers are a provider's company image, price and value, and power quality and reliability. As expected, midsize business customers were extremely critical of performance in the areas of company image and price and value. Even outside of California, satisfaction in the price and value component fell more than any other factor -- from an index score of 100 in 2000 to 93 in 2001.
