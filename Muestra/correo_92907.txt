Last week, authorities charged Abdelkader Smires with launching denial- of-service attacks against his own employer, New York-based Internet Trading Technologies, Inc. (ITTI). Smires was upset that ITTI did not meet his demands for a raise and more stock options. He used computers at a Kinko's Copy store and a local university to launch his revenge attacks against ITTI that disrupted the company's trading systems for three days.
