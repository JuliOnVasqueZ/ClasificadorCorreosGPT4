With fourth quarter OPEC crude output at 29.5 MMB/D, world onshore oil inventories should draw only 100-200 MB/D in the quarter. Thus assuming normal early winter weather, stocks will remain very low but not, on average, decline significantly. For the three major OECD markets, commercial inventories should end the year 1-2% above the year earlier. In these circumstances and given the very positive market factors noted above, it is hard to see oil prices taking a big hit downward as a result of the OPEC agreement.
