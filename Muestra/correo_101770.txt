We will use a technique called "ongoing valuation" to allow non-residentials to elect other suppliers while still receiving the benefits/paying the costs of our current supplies. We are planning to be in balance (or long) for 2002 on a full load basis. Thus, while non-residential customers may elect new suppliers, they are essentially fully hedged with our portfolio of long- and short-term supplies.
