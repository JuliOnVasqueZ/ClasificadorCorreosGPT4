11/06/2000 12:16 PM Have you heard of something called "Track changes"? It's a WORD feature that is used -- a lot -- for redlining drafts. I see it used all the time on documents that I receive from 3rd parties and I use it myself. It has become popular because other tools, like CompareWrite and similar software, are not compatible w/ more recent versions of WordPerfect and WORD, so changes are often missed in marked drafts I learned during a recent visit at my old law firm that that they've forbidden use of "Track Changes" and are advising their clients
