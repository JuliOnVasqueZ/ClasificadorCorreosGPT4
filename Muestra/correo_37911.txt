With regard to whether QFs need power marketing authorization, most QFs are exempt (some small power production facilities are not exempt) from most provisions of the FPA, including section 205, which requires public utilities making sales of electric energy for resale in interstate commerce to file rate schedules with FERC. QFs are instead regulated under PURPA, which authorizes utilities to purchase power from QFs at the purchaser's avoided cost.
