(C) Reuters Limited 2001. BOMBAY, June 4 (Reuters) - The rupee ended unchanged on Monday, recovering from the day's low after a large European bank sold dollars, dealers said. It had slipped to a low of 47.04 per dollar in early deals on dollar purchases by a jewellery importer. The rupee ended at steady at d47.01/02 per dollar. Dealers said there was no impact of Friday's comments from international rating agencies which had raised concerns over continuity of foreign investment and dragged shares lower.
