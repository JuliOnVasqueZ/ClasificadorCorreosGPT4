It's basically a physical product that is transacted on a day-ahead cash basis, but is delivered on real-time. For example, on the day-ahead, lets say I post a "US Pwr Phy Firm Mid-C Peak Oct04 to Oct05 DART" $27 at $28. If I get hit, then the party selling to me is obligated to deliver on Oct 5th, real-time 25 MW each hour. I would be long real-time at $27.
