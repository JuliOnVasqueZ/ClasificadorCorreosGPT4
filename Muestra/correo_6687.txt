With rare exceptions, Texas' so-called three-tier system kept brewers, wholesalers and retailers from joint ownership or ventures. It prevented a few companies from dominating the alcoholic-beverage industry from fermentation to final sale. The law did not keep a handful of companies from dominating the wholesaling segment of the industry.
