As a result, demand curves have been categorized as 'inelastic' or 'vertical' suggesting the need for 'capacity' related products to meet any unexpected price insensitive demand. The creation of RTOs and the resulting spot markets they include will, for the first time, give consumers the means and incentive to change the shape of their demand curve as a result of direct real time pricing information.
