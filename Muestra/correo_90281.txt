2001. US Inventory Highlights * Primary inventories of unleaded gasoline rose to 196.5 mb, increasing almost 10 mb from the end-October value. Responding to rising stock levels and declining apparent demand, forward supply coverage of gasoline climbed to 23.5 days for the four weeks ending December 8. Forward supply is 2.5 percent below last year's level at this time of year and at its lowest level in the past eight years-although CERA still believes coverage is currently sufficient for the level of demand expected this winter.
