Principal Investments will be to work with the all the business units of Enron to identify, execute, and manage equity investments, which leverage the Enron's unique and proprietary knowledge. These investments may be in the form of venture capital, LBO's, traditional private equity and distressed debt positions Kevin Garland will serve as Managing Director, overseeing all activities of Enron Principal Investments. Gene Humphrey, Michael Miller, Dick Lydecker, and their groups, will join Kevin and his group to form this new investments unit. This new group will report to an investment committee consisting of Greg Whalley, Ken Rice, and Dave Delainey.
