My concern is that such purported "set-off" is so different in substance from the common notion of set-off which requires mutuality, as to be set-off in name only. Without mutuality, I am concerned that a court may not recognize such "set-off" of obligations relating to third parties as "set-off" within the meaning of Canadian insolvency legislation.
