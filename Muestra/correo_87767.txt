Also last month, IntercontinentalExchange in Atlanta, whose partners include BP Amoco and The Hague-based Royal Dutch/Shell Group, added natural gas and power to its online trading system, which already included precious metals and crude oil. Skilling doesn't get called "nuts" much anymore. In fact, he's a darling on Wall Street, where Enron's stock has soared from around $40 per share last November to $81 as of this Nov. 2. As a result, Enron - by all accounts, an Old Economy gas pipeline company until 12 months ago - is widely regarded as a New Economy pioneer.
