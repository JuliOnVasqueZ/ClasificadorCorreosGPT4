WeaponX imports and deletes a lot of data on a daily basis. When data is deleted, the space is not freed up that was used by the data. Therefore, as time goes by, the database size grows and grows and needs to be periodically compacted. The Texas WeaponX file that I compacted reduced from 520 MB to 30 MB and one for the west went from 400 MB to 12 MB.
