HOUSTON (Dow Jones)--High natural gas prices could slow the stampede to build as much as 140,000 megawatts of new electric generation across the U.S. and give new coal technology the boost it needs to compete economically with gas in the future. While market observers say the recent level of the price of natural gas - above $4.00 per million British thermal unit since late May - may not be sustainable, many expect gas to remain above $3.00/MMBtu for the foreseeable future.
