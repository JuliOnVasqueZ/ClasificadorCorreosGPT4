September 7th at 2:30 p.m. On July 26th, FERC issued a Notice of Proposed Rulemaking (NOPR) proposing to replace its current filing requirements for short- and long-term service agreements with an electronically-filed Index of Customers, among other changes. Comments are due to FERC on or before October 5th. On August 8th, EPSA sent out a memo briefly summarizing the Order and identifying some possible issues of concern. If you have any additional issues, please forward them to Jackie Gallagher at jgallagher@epsa.org.
