In the revised agreement, the city also would receive $200,000 a year, use of the arena for convention-related events or fund-raisers on 20 days a year, and a suite between the free-throw lines. It's a "much better deal" for Houston, says Mayor Lee Brown, who, as a chief negotiator and supporter of the previous deal, is among the least likely to make such admissions. City officials have valued the suite at $250,000 to $500,000 because it would allow Houston to woo convention center customers. The Rockets plan to sell the suites for $100,000 to $150,000 each.
