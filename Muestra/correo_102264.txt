According to a three-year study by the Office of Admissions, students carrying 15-17 hours outperformed their classmates taking 12-13 hours. Nevertheless, the portion of students attempting 14-17 hours is decreasing (from 47.7% in 1996 to about 39% in 1999). The correlation between 15-17 hour course load and a higher GPA was true in all racial/ethnic groups and despite differences in academic ability as measured by the SAT/ACT tests.
