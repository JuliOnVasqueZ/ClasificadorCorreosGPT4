Generators receive three payments, the reserve payments mentioned above, a capacity payment, and an energy payment. PJM, like ECAR, has a need for operating reserves and purchases these in order to maintain reliability. Additionally, the PJM model provides an Unforced Capacity payment to all generators based on their historical performance.
