At midnight every night, the system checks the current date and then CHANGES the dates shown on the Product Description. For example, if Product #20451 is "Month+1", then on Feb. 28, the Short Description on the Website and in Stack Manager would show "April1-30 However, on March 1, the system will automatically change the date of Product #20451 to show "May1-31".
