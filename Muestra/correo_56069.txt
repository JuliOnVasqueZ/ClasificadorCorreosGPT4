The reliance argument is that we excused other bidders weeks ago and chose to exclusively deal with FWL based on agreement (reliance) of terms and conditions presented in earlier drafts of the PO or referred to in previous PO documents. The detriment argument is FWL knew they had us captive to them on this deal. By going forward without other bidders, we lost price protection and commitment for schedule by other bidders, therefore we are at a detriment without market forces. In the best world, our purchasing activities need to keep proposals competitive by not dropping back to one supplier
