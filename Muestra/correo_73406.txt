The Pinnacle Index is designed to measure the level of overhead resistance at key overhead benchmark levels. This analysis can give savvy option traders a jump on other traders. It's important to note that the Pinnacle Index, like many sentiment indicators, is a contrarian indictor. This means that the stock can and will go higher or lower despite what our sentiment indicators might suggest. However, our experience has shown that the market is subject to a precipitous sell-off if the Pinnacle Index reaches extreme levels.
