The Wall Street Journal 03/07/2001 The Wall Street Journal A2 WASHINGTON -- Angry and confused that a Republican administration has targeted one of their favorite programs for big cuts, America's largest exporters are appealing for help from lawmakers. Boeing Co., Caterpillar Inc., Enron Corp., Halliburton Co. and others are launching an aggressive lobbying campaign on Capitol Hill to protect the Export-Import Bank from budget reductions; the White House labels the Ex-Im Bank as corporate welfare. "The administration has fired its shot, and now we're firing ours," said Edmund B. Rice, president of the Coalition for Employment Through Exports, an industry
