Please be notified that the annual meeting of the Shareholders of Subic Power Corp. will be held on 25 April 2001 at 2:00 p.m. at the 3rd Floor Board Room, Shangri-La Hotel, Ayala Avenue, City of Makati, Metro Manila, Philippines. The agenda of the meeting is as follows: Certification of notice and quorum; Approval of the minutes of the Shareholders' meeting held on 22 June 2000; Appointment of External Auditors; Ratification of acts and proceedings of the Board of Directors and Officers; Election of the Board of Directors; and Approval of other matters as may properly come before the Shareholders.
