BASEBALL RECRUITING NOTES: Michael Hollimon, 6-1, 170, of Dallas Jesuit, the state's top shortstop, and Ryan Olivo, 6-2, 175, of Grapevine, one of the state's top second basemen, have committed to Texas. Hollimon hit .400 last season and also was recruited by Stanford, LSU, Baylor and Texas A&M. He narrowed his choices to Texan and Stanford and committed to the Longhorns Monday night after visiting Stanford last weekend.
