Niagara Mohawk Holdings Inc. is an investor-owned energy services company that provides electricity to more than 1.5 million customers across 24,000 square miles of Upstate New York. The company also delivers natural gas to more than 540,000 customers over 4,500 square miles of eastern, central, and northern New York. National Grid USA includes local electric companies Massachusetts Electric, Narragansett Electric, Granite State Electric, Nantucket Electric, and a substantial transmission business. Its parent company, National Grid Group plc, builds, owns, and operates electric and telecommunications networks around the world.
