The bill also would make Chapter 12 for farmers a permanent part of the bankruptcy code. The package strengthens this tool for farmers by making an increased number of farmers eligible for Chapter 12 protections and making the federal government last on the list of creditors when a farmer sells farm assets during reorganization.
