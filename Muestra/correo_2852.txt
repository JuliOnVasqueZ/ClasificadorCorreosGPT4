Most of the U.S.' busiest airports have implemented the FAA's stepped-up security measures and are open and operating, with the exception of Washington Reagan National Airport. If you are traveling by air, allow plenty of time to check in under these new security procedures. Contact your carrier directly to find out about flight schedules and specific boarding requirements.
