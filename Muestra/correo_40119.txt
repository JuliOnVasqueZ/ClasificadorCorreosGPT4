Portland General, which provides electric service to over 700,000 customers in the Portland-Salem area, was viewed as a good acquisition target as it would help Sierra Pacific Resources to gain scale and a foothold in two fast-growing states (Nevada and Oregon). Sierra Pacific Resources agreed to pay Enron $2.1 billion for Portland General, which included $2.02 billion in cash and assumption of Enron's $80 million merger payment obligation.
