Lisa Kammert, an analyst at Birinyi Associates, has been tracking two portfolios comprising 12 stocks each that would do well under Gore or Bush. As of Monday, Gore's portfolio has outperformed Bush's by 4.43 percent, based on a simple average of each stock's return. In 1992, the same model accurately predicted a Clinton win. In the Gore portfolio are Amazon.com, Yahoo, Citigroup, Bank of America, Disney, Time Warner, BellSouth, Duke Energy, Tenet Healthcare, Procter Gamble, Coca-Cola and Waste Management.
