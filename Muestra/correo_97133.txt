Methanol margins are also suffering with current gas prices, even given recent healthy methanol prices. Methanol prices are being supported by the return of Asian demand; specific plant outages, including several ammonia feeder plants in Trinidad; and the late startup of several world-scale methanol plants, including plants in Trinidad, Saudi Arabia, and Iran.
