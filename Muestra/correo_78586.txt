Customers can also run reports to see their own transactions and download the results into an Excel spreadsheet on EnronOnline The system also allows for administrative users, thereby allowing the back offices of counterparties to monitor the trading activity and deal with it appropriately in line with their own systems. Enron does not publish transactions completed via EnronOnline however Enron will provide information on such transactions in order to ensure market activity is being correctly reflected in the approriate market
