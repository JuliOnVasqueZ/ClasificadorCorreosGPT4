the IECC Energy Code The North Central Texas Council of Governments (NCTCOG) voted this month to adopt the 2000 International Energy Conservation Code (IECC) for the Dallas region. This area accounts for almost 50,000 housing starts a year, more than are built in 42 states. One of the main reasons for adopting the code is the area's commitment to reduce nitrogen oxide emissions as part of the Texas state plan for controlling smog.
