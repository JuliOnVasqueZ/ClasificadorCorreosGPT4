Breach Of Power Sales Pact Cost $140 Mln By Bryan Lee 10/16/2000 Dow Jones Energy Service OF DOW JONES NEWSWIRES WASHINGTON -(Dow Jones)- The nation's top two power providers are battling in a U.S. court over an unexecuted power sales agreement. The Tennessee Valley Authority (TVA) has filed a claim against the power marketing unit of Enron Corp. (ENE) alleging breach of a power sales contract. TVA cited the alleged breach of contract in documents provided to its distribution utility customers earlier this month outlining unforeseen expenses contributing to the government-owned utility's inability to meet its debt-reduction forecast.
