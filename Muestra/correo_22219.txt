The traditional way to get an extension is to fill out IRS Form 4868, "Application for Automatic Extension of Time to File U.S. Individual Income Tax Return" and mail it to the IRS by the April 16 deadline. If you're using tax-preparation software or a tax professional, you can file your extension request by computer. Or you can phone in your extension request by calling 888-796-1074.
