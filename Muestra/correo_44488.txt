The workshop, funded under the auspices of a National Science Foundation grant to SIAM will not be a standard applied mathematics event with representatives from industry, academe, and governmental agencies presenting their latest research results. Instead the meeting will emphasize the mathematics and technology currently applied to the projects of industry and governmental laboratories. Additionally the event will focus upon the mechanisms facilitating interaction and collaboration between the academy, industry, and government laboratories.
