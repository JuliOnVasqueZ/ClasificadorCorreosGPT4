The online and offline worlds came together today in another cross-marketing alliance, this one between Pepsi-Cola (NYSE:PEP) and Yahoo! (Nasdaq:YHOO). The companies announced a joint online and offline promotional campaign called "Pepsi Stuff.com" that will last 5 months beginning in August. The program will enable consumers to earn online awards and discounts from promotions appearing under the caps of bottled Pepsi products. A Yahoo! logo will also appear in close to 50,000 retail stores
