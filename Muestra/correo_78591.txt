The transactions are 'bridged' (duplicated) onto the various systems (databases) managing the underlying commodity (the risk system associated with the commodity). We currently intend to store the data for a period of six years post a transaction completing (for example, deliveries finishing) and this is policy across all databases. In general, EnronOnline is an electronic trading platform that offers free, real-time pricing information for approximately 1,800 products for 13 commodities, including electricity and natural gas.
