about "Operation Blue TALON 3: Crash Crackdown," which will occur over the Labor Day weekend. More than 74,000 law enforcement officers will join together to help prevent injury and death in motor vehicle crashes by enforcing traffic laws for occupant protection use, drinking and driving, aggressive driving, and exceeding speed limits. Law enforcement officers will sweep Interstates 10, 30, 35, 40, and their feeder routes in the states of Texas, Arkansas, Louisiana, Oklahoma, New Mexico, and many of the Indian Nations.
