Because of the bursty and intermittent nature of IP traffic, "oversubscription" is possible In telephone or cell phone networks, selling more lines than the physical capacity is commonplace. When too many users access the lines at 5pm, busy signals result. With service level agreements that call for damages to be paid when capacity is unavailable, simple busy signals will not do. An approach to this issue and thoughts on the trade off between oversubscription and liquidated damages will be the focus of this interesting talk.
