CATEX initially planned to be a computer-based facility for reinsurers that would enable them to exchange catastrophe risks and to build balanced portfolios. But by the time the exchange was operational in November 1996, it was clear that most reinsurers and insurers interested in CATEX wanted only to buy and sell conventional reinsurance, Mr. Sweeney said.
