Gas Storage--Not Yet Enough Despite recent high storage injection rates, storage inventories are not high enough to ensure that supplies will reach adequate levels for next winter. An end of mild weather in the Midwest and Northeast and seasonally intense heat in the Southwest would cause injection rates to decline significantly over the remainder of July and into August.
