This is a sale and buy back structure whereby Enron sells on a border basis to a Dutch distribution company who then sells back to Enron a proportion of the power on a free on Koppelnet basis. The Koppelnet is the Dutch grid. The deals are designed to secure as much cross border capacity to take advantage of the still significant arbitrage between power prices within the Netherlands and those outside.
