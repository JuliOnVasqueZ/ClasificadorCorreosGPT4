ELECTRICAL GENERATION - EMINENT DOMAIN ABX2 35 (Vargas D-San Diego) authorizes the California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority to exercise eminent domain to acquire generating facilities for 18-24 months. Generators would be reimbursed a "reasonable rental value" as opposed to "fair market value" for facilities temporarily taken by the State. Co-generation facilities are not exempt from the bill.
