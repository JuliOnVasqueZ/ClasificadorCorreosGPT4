Nuclear plants deliver about 20 percent of the nation's electricity but construction of new nuclear plants is widely viewed as politically impossible following the accidents at Three Mile Island in 1979 and at Chernobyl in 1986. Natural gas currently supplies about 15 percent of U.S. electricity but is projected to gain market share steadily due to power generators' enthusiasm for the high efficiency and low emissions of the latest "combined-cycle" gas turbines.
