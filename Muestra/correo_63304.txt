Enron said Monday that the Securities and Exchange Commission was asking questions about partnerships run by Andrew Fastow, who was ousted as Enron's chief financial officer yesterday. One partnership cost the company $35 million, and Enron bought back 62 million shares from a partnership at a cost of $1.2 billion. Enron named Jeff McMahon, head of its industrial markets group, as CFO late yesterday.
