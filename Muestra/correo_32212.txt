In fact, Duquesne and the rest of the ECAR area ensure reliability through different, equally effective but lower-cost reliability rules. The Current ECAR Model The ECAR model could be summarized by the phrase "pay only for what you need." The current reliability mechanism is met through purchasing 4 percent operating reserves.
