Ray Alvarez 04/25/2001 11:58 PM The following report is comprised of what was discussed at the Commission meeting held tonight at approximately 7 PM EST and additional intelligence gathered from FERC staffers after the meeting by Joe Hartsoe: Physical Withholding -- To prevent physical withholding, the plan will require sellers with PGA's to offer all their available power in real time All California generators, even those not subject to FERC price regulation, will be required to sell into the ISO's real time market as a condition of their use of the ISO's interstate transmission lines. Hydroelectric facilities will be exempted.
