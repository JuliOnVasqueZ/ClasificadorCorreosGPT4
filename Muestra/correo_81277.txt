Ahead/Real-Time Park/Lend product. Essentially it's a financial trade on a day ahead basis that is settled real time. For example, let's say Morgan Stanley agrees to sell us 25 MW onpeak for $25, but that it is not prescheduled--it is to be settled real time. If we fast forward to real-time, Morgan Stanley has two options. They can buy from us on EOL, whereby this transaction books out, or they can deliver us physical power, whereby we have to sell the power to a third party real time.
