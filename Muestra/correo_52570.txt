"Thus, no matter how good or bad the actual performance of employees a certain number of them must still receive the lower scores." Any employee with a 2.5 score is placed on a "personal improvement plan" or asked to resign, the complaint said. "Stack rankings" are used to rank employees in the same job category and organizational unit from best to worst, the complaint said. Employees can be ranked higher under the stack ranking than others who receive numerically higher performance evaluations, the lawsuit said.
