Johnston 10/27/2000 10:07 AM We continue to monitor the oil/Euro on two fronts. With respect to Iraq, the US has dropped its opposition to allowing Baghdad to receive its oil revenues from the UN oil-for-food program in euros rather than dollars. This means that the estimated 10 billion $US in Iraq's BNP account could be converted to euros in the next 30 days, perhaps all at once. We are looking into the exact timing and process for this conversion. Per our report on Wednesday about Welteke lobbying the Saudis to convert their oil trading to euros, we await further information
