Given these products prices, gas prices of $5.00 in the Gulf Coast and $4.25 in the Rockies would be required before processors leave significant quantities of ethane in the gas stream. In addition, propane, the major alternate to ethane in the petrochemical sector, is currently in short supply. The result is that until world oil! markets loosen, allowing imports of propane, ethane is likely to follow gas upward, should gas even reach the ethane price.
