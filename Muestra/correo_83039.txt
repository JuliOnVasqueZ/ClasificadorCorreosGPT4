We should see a modest recovery late this year, and a pick-up of steam in 2002. The California economy is in for a rougher time, at least in the short term. And this time -- unlike the early 1990s, when Southern California bore the brunt of the severe downturn in the defense industry -- the Bay Area will be hit much harder. While the region grew much faster than the rest of the state in the '90s, most of the symbols of that growth -- full employment, exploding property values, shrinking commercial vacancy rates -- are already history.
