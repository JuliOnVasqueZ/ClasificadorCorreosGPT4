As the money piled up, utilities used it to pay down debts for generation facilities that were otherwise unprofitable in the new deregulated world. By the end of this June, PG&E and Edison together had collected more than $12 billion and were on track to finish paying down debts well ahead of the March 31, 2002, deadline set by the legislature.
