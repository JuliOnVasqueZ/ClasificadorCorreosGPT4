Pete eventually turns up again, only to become perturbed when Everett reveals that his real goal, rather than the nonexistent treasure, is to reunite with the mother of his seven daughters, Penny (Holly Hunter), who is about to marry another fellow. In the course of things, the errant adventurers brush up against local politics, a governor's race that pits old incumbent Pappy O'Daniel (Charles Durning) against a reform candidate whose motto, ``Friend of the Little Man,'' is literally represented by a broom-toting dwarf who accompanies him at every campaign stop.
