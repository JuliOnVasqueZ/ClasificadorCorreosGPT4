Pricier gas could create a variety of effects on future electric generation: from damping interest in building some new gas-fired plants to improving economic prospects for investment to upgrade or build new coal-fired plants. Current gas prices are off the chart when compared to existing forecasts from the Energy Information Institute, part of the U.S. Department of Energy.
