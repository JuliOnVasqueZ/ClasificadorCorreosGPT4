Comptroller of Public Accounts has initially ruled that REPs will be responsible for administering the taxes. This conclusion was based upon the assumption that the REPs are purchasing the T&D Services from the TDU and then reselling the services to their customers. After a number of REPs objected to that ruling, the Comptroller's office agreed to reconsider. We believe the Comptroller is worried about doing something that will have a serious impact on deregulation in Texas, so she is passing the question off to the Texas PUC by requesting that the PUC determine whether REPs purchase and resell T&D Services.
