According to their findings, Most Admired companies set more challenging goals, link the compensation of their executives more closely to the completion of those goals, and are generally more oriented toward long-term performance. To complete the list of the world's most admired companies, FORTUNE consulted the experts: the business executives who run companies, and the analysts who study them. Global companies were rated according to eight criteria: quality of management; quality of products or services; innovativeness; long-term investment value; financial soundness; ability to attract, develop and retain talent; community responsibility; and use of corporate assets.
