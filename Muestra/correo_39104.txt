Business-to-business (B2B) news was topped by reports that four major players in the aerospace and defense industry are joining forces to create an online marketplace with B2B powerhouse Commerce One (Nasdaq:CMRC). The joint venture, which includes Lockheed Martin (NYSE:LMT), Raytheon (NYSE:RTNa), Boeing (NYSE:BA) and British Aerospace, is being designed to bring together buyers and suppliers in the industry, allowing them to buy and sell parts and conduct business over the Internet.
