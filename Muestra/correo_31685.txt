The provision covers brief illnesses such as colds, high temperature, sore throats etc., that are more likely to be associated with children and is less likely to involve physician or hospital care. However, employers opposed the bill because many employees take advantage of the easy threshold and use it to avoid workplace sanctions. When employees don't show up or show up late for work, it negatively impacts production. In order to mitigate this problem, employers have implemented absentee control programs that may include counseling, suspension and termination.
