Consensus The proceeds should be allocated between the debt liability and the warrant and the resulting discount should be amortized in accordance with Opinion 21. The warrants should be considered, in substance, debt and accounted for as a liability because the settlement alternatives for the warrants do not have the same economic value attached to them and they provide the holder with a guaranteed return in cash that is significantly in excess of the value of the share settlement alternative on the issuance date.
