The commission also recognized that market activities need not all fall under the rubric of a stand-alone RTO. For instance, it stated, the Midwest ISO, Alliance and the Southwest Power Pool have proposed forming one market-monitoring unit. The order paid special attention to inviting the participation of state regulators in developing viable RTO structures.
