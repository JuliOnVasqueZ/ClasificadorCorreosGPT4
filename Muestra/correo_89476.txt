Is Enron too big and too important to be allowed to fail? Philip Verleger, an energy economist, thinks that Enron is so central to energy markets that it could not easily be replaced. Enron's rivals mostly disagree, unsurprisingly. But even Enron's worst enemies do not (yet) expect the firm to die from its current crisis. Most traders seem keen that it should live.
