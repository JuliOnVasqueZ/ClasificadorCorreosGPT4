just returned from the Bond Market Association meeting this afternoon on the pending bankruptcy legislation. Chris Long (Enron) was there with Ellen Levinson of our Washington office. The "big" issue discussed was whether to push hard for amendments on cross-affiliate netting. The consensus was that while everyone in the room (and all of Wall Street was there,as well as ISDA and commodities groups) wants the amendments, they are considered controversial by the President's Working Group and could derail progress on the version of bankruptcy reform currently before Congress which everyone also agrees is very good legislation for financial markets participants.
