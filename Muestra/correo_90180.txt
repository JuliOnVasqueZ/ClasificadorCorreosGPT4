Since prices weakened in late November, crude oil stocks have stabilized above the annual low in September and the ranges seen in October to levels from 289-292 million barrels. Primary inventories of US heating oil remain well below year-earlier levels; at 48 million barrels they are 15 million barrels, or 24 percent less, than those of a year ago, but there are indications of builds in secondary and tertiary inventories.
