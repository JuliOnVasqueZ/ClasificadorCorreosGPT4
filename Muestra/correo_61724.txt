The first black man pledged a traditionally white fraternity in 1984, and there currently is one black pledge among those 24 fraternities. Auburn's overall black population is still less than at most Southeastern Conference universities, but stepped-up recruiting efforts led to a 24 percent increase of African-Americans admitted to this year's freshman class.
