Enron is the principal in nearly one-quarter of all electricity and natural gas trades in the United States. For Enron's power and gas trading counterparties, a credit event such as a downgrade by Moody's may precipitate certain obligations under the terms of their trading transactions. For example, any downgrade, not just a downgrade resulting in a sub-investment grade rating, may entitle a counterparty to require Enron to provide additional credit in support of Enron's purchase obligations.
