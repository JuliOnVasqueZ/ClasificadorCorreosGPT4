By 2002, the Polish government proposes to sell off stakes in dozens of power plants, 33 distribution companies and the Polish Power Grid, a 12,000-kilometer national transmission network of high-voltage lines. The assets have a total book value of 33 billion zlotys (8.4 billion euros), but murky regulations have driven prices down.
