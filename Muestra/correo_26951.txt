The plants supply approximately 40% of the electrical power capacity in the Chicago area. They are: Crawford Station: 3501 S. Pulaski Road, Chicago. Fisk Station: 1111 W. Cermak Road, Chicago. Collins Station: N. side of Pine Bluff Road, 4 miles east of IL Rt. 47, Morris. Joliet Station, #9: 1601 S. Patterson, Joliet. Joliet Station, #29: 1800 Channahon Road, Joliet. Powerton Station: 2 miles south of Pekin, off IL Rt. 29 on Manito Road, Pekin. Waukegan Station: 10 Greenwood Ave., Waukegan. Will County Station: 529 E. Romeo Road, Romeoville. In 1999, Midwest Generation acquired the plants from Commonwealth Edison Co.
