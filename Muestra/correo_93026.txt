For instance, using just an Intel 486 PC with a floppy drive, 12M bytes of RAM and at least two network interface cards, LRP can be used to assemble a 10M bit/sec Ethernet router. For higher speeds, a Fast Ethernet router can be assembled with a simple Pentium 100 or higher, along with 16M bytes of RAM and 10/100M bit/sec network interface cards.
