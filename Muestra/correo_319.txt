Overview Designs and markets air cooled heat exchangers. GEA Rainey is a wholly owned subsidiary of GEA AG (the world's largest heat transfer equipment company). All manufacturing is done within facility Business Areas: Design and fabrication of heat transfer equipment including; forced and induced draft air cooled heat exchangers, hydrocarbon process and steam condensers, engine radiators, turbine lube oil coolers, natural gas and vapor coolers, combustion pre-heaters, and flue gas re-heaters. After market products and services including; replacement air moving parts, replacement finned tubes, replacement finned tubes bundles, field service thermal performance evaluation, field service noise testing, field service vibration
