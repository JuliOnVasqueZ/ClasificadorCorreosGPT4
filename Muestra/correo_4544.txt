Giving someone all your love is never assurance that they'll love you
 back!  Don't expect love in return; just wait for it to grow in their
 heart but if it doesn't, be content it grew in yours.  There are things
 you'd love to hear that you would never hear from the person whom you
 would like to hear them from, but don't be so deaf as not to hear it from
 the one who says it from his heart.
