11, 2001 Commentary Even Pacifists Must Support This War By Scott Simon, the host of National Public Radio's "Weekend Edition With Scott Simon." Pacifists often commit the same mistake as generals: They prepare for the last war, not the next one. Many of the peace activists I have seen trying to rouse opposition to today's war against terrorism remind me of a Halloween parade. They put on old, familiar-looking protest masks -- against American imperialism, oppression and violence -- that bear no resemblance to the real demons haunting us now.
