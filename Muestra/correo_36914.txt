- heat.exe Lorraine, I've been studying the El Paso/Dynegy reservation reduction mechanism (RRM). Basically the deal was that Dynegy's minimum-pay obligation would be reduced by the amount of the fixed cost component of IT revenues generated by El Paso's transportation of IT volumes above a historical threshold. (The parties based the threshold on El Paso's monthly IT volumes for the 12 months ended 9/30/97.) The volumes in excess of the threshold were calculated and a ratio of the volumes in excess of the threshold to the total volumes was determined; the ratio would then be applied to the fixed cost
