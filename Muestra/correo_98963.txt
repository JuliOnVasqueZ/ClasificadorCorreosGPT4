Under a ruling yesterday by state regulators, California taxpayers could be on the hook for hundreds of millions of dollars in power purchases the state has made in behalf of two troubled utilities. The California Public Utilities Commission voted unanimously to absolve Pacific Gas and Electric Co. and Southern California Edison of responsibility for any costs above the revenue they collect from ratepayers.
