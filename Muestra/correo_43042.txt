The 200-plus pages report draws a detailed road map for the need to privatise MSEB, a different model from what was implemented in other states, sources added. The sources said experiences of Orissa, Haryana, Karnataka and Andhra Pradesh were closely studied by the panel and then it decided to recommend this model of privatisation. "The mantra of privatisation of only distribution network has failed all over the country", official sources said. The report has emphasised on creating distribution network zones in the state's urban and rural areas
