Reserve payments compensate only those units that are not operating at the time but have the capability of generating in less than 10 minutes. The cost for these reserves is not easily calculated due to lack of a transparent market. Yet they appear to be approximately less than $2/MW-day during on-peak periods and often less than $1/MW-day.
