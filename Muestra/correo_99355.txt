Thanks Mike Hicks Enron's policy is to periodically remind offices of procedures to follow for proper screening of mail. If an x-ray machine is available in your office, all incoming mail and couriered packages should be scanned. If no x-ray machine is available, you should scan you mail visually, looking for the following: - Priority markings such as 'Rush', 'Personal', or 'Confidential' - Excessive postage - False or missing return address - Poorly typed or handwritten address - Incorrect titles - Addressed to titles, but no
