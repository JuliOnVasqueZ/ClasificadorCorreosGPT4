Having several people (but not too many) around this time is helpful to put food away, take inventory, attend boat briefing, etc. Of course, if you get the boat the day before - maybe with a sleepaboard option or some such, you have that much more certainty that everything will be done on time. Check with your charter company about attending the briefings before sending the last person off to the airport - some companies may require 2 people from your boat to attend.
