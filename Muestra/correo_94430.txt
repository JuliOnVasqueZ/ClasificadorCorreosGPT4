Thursday, 22 February 2001. Professor Elsner is a leading expert in hurricane climate studies. who is currently investigating interannual/decadal/multi-decadal hurricane variations and shifts/trends in hurricane landfall probabilities and their relationship to climate and climate change; he also issues seasonal predictions for number of hurricanes and landfall probabilities along segments of the U.S. coastline. Moreover, he is a leading authority in non-linear dynamics and chaos theory and has published a number of papers on these topics His talk will focus on a proposed research topic, which will provide Enron with climatological instruments for use in the insurance and long-term derivative markets.
