Lieutenant Governor Jane Swift explained that the agreement resulted through "cooperative efforts," but added, "We will pursue new regulations through the Department of Environmental Protection to ensure the plant owners' commitments are enforceable, and will require the finalization of a detailed plan for each of the six facilities." The regulations, which are expected to be proposed in the next few months -- will set standards of 1.5 ponds of nitrogen oxide and 3 pounds of sulfur dioxide per megawatt-hour.
