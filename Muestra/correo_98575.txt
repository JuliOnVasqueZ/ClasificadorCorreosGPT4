Two other towers also are underway. In 1999, Hines began building a 1.2 million square-foot tower for Enron Corp. and late last year Crescent Real Estate Equities broke ground on a 27-story tower that will be anchored by Ernst & Young. These three projects are the first major downtown office projects to get underway since 1986.
