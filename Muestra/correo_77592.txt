The PUC provides the following example, if a utility generates and purchases 750 MWh of power and delivers along with it 250 MWh of DWR power for a total of 1,000 MWh, each customer owes DWR 25% of the energy charge in their rates for the power they used that day. Utilities will collect these amounts from customers on behalf of DWR and hold the amounts in trust for DWR.
