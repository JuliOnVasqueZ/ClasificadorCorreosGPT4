By CURT ANDERSON AP Tax Writer 10/19/2000 Associated Press Newswires WASHINGTON (AP) - Dozens of America's most profitable companies enjoyed tax-free years during the 1990s largely because of legal tax breaks, an independent study released Thursday found. The report by the nonprofit Institute on Taxation and Economic Policy found that 250 companies paid an effective tax rate of 20.1 percent in 1998, down from 22.9 percent just two years before.
