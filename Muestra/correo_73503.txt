Xilinx's chips are used by electronics makers in the telecommunications, data processing, industrial and medical instrumentation, military and aerospace, and networking markets. The company minimizes its overhead by contracting out its chip manufacturing to companies in Asia. About a quarter of Xilinx's sales are in Europe. Main rival Altera has a nearly equal market share.
