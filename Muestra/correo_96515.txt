Vince Chonawee, As I have pointed out, short-selling the stock may be a bad decision because of tax implications (ignoring the legal aspects). Suppose the strike is $70 and you were granted an ATM option. You sell short at $70 ten lots (one lot = 100 shares). The price goes to $100 You lose $30 x 1000 = $30,000 on your short position. Option exercise gives you $30,000. This is before taxes. You pay taxes on your option income (it's treated as ordinary income). The tax is 28% x $30,000 = $8,400.
