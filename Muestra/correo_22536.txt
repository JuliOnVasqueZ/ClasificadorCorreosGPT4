The Policy also focuses on four major types of risk: (a) market risk; (b) credit risk; (c) operational risk and (d) liquidity/funding risk. These risks will be managed on a portfolio basis with standard transactions being subject to various market risk limit structures and credit approval processes and non-standard transactions being subject to the Transaction Approval Process and the Guarantee Approval Process.
