Houston, Oct. 25 (Bloomberg) -- Concerns Enron Corp. will run short on cash haven't prevented commodities traders from doing business with the largest energy broker, customers say. The company handles about 25 percent of U.S. power and natural-gas trading, said John Kilduff, vice president of energy risk management for Fimat USA. It's also a leader in complex derivatives that allow others to hedge against the risk of fluctuating commodities prices.
