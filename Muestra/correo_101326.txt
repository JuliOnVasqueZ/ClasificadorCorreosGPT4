The new process combines use of a "transport reactor," a device based on existing technology used to "crack" crude oil into lighter products, to turn coal into a gas which is then burned in a combustion turbine much like natural gas. In its gaseous form, coal can be cleaned of many of its pollutant-forming impurities such as sulfur dioxide, nitrogen oxides and particulate matter, Goodman said.
