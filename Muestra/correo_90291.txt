memorandum of understanding to transport, market natural gas Regional, Economics, 12/16/2000 In the presence of the Lebanese prime minister Rafik al- Hariri a memorandum of understanding was signed on Friday in Beirut between Syria, Egypt and Lebanon on the transportation and marketing of natural gas. On the Syrian side the memo. Was signed by minister of oil Maher Jammal and minister of oil in Egypt Sameh Fahmi and the Lebanese side was attended by Muhammad Abdul hamid Beydoun, the minister of energy and waters, in the presence of the secretary general of the Syrian- Lebanese higher council Nasr al-Kouri.
