US holding company. For each company we will need to find out the following info which will need to be notified to SFA: 1. Principal activities 2. Date and place of incorporation and registered number 3. Names of directors and their dates of birth 4. Address of head/principal office and telephone number 5. Regulatory Authorities or Exchanges in the UK or overseas of which the entity is or has been a member. Most of this info is pretty basic, but I suspect 1 &5 could be slightly tricky (for 1 we only need to list the principal activity or activities).
