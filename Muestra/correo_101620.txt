The utilities' stealth campaign is one reason energy deregulation legislation has been stalled, although last fall one partial deregulation bill was reported to the full committee. Commerce Chairman Thomas J. Bliley Jr. (R-Va.) is a staunch industry critic determined to move a bill. The documents describe meetings in Washington steak houses such as Sam & Harry's where utility lobbyists and consultants shaped strategies for stopping Bliley. The dozen or so participants in the operation--who called their effort "The Project"--address each other in the memos with the kind of blunt talk that isn't often found in civics
