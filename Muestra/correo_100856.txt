"We hope our findings will encourage lawmakers to re-examine this important area of taxation," McIntyre said. The report also found that the petroleum industry paid the lowest tax rate from 1996-98, at 12.3 percent, followed by electronics at 13.1 percent, forest and paper products at 13.9 percent and transportation at 14 percent. At the other end of the spectrum, publishing and printing companies paid 31.6 percent, gas and electric utilities paid 28.1 percent and retail and wholesale trade companies paid 27.6 percent.
