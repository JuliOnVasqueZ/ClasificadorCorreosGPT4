Judge Damrell took each motion under submission and stated that he will issue an order on or before February 21, 2001 at 5:00 p.m. He ordered the February 8, 2001 TRO to remain in effect until then. Judge Damrell clarified that the TRO requires Defendants to respond to all ISO Emergency Dispatch Orders ("EDOs") regardless of the creditworthiness of the purchasers. The Judge encouraged Defendants to stipulate that they would respond to ISO EDOs, and gave Defendants until February 20, 2001 to do so. (It appears that only AES is willing to do so at this time).
