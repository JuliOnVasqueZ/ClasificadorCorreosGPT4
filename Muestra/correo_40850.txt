baseload plant Someone on the phone last week had some numbers indicating that the DOE/DOD electric load at Kirtland had a fairly high load factor. I think we have all assumed further that the plant should run at a high load factor to sell surplus power into the grid (either at 4 Corners or, after N.M. elec. restructuring, into the Alb. area). I just found the numbers I was remembering on the conf. call--1998 peak load was 63.6 mw, and total annual 1998 consumption was 334.5 million Kwh. By my lawyer-math, that is about a 60% load factor for DOE.
