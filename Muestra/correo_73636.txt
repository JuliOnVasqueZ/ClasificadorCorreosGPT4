Chart INDEX OPTION SPREADS As a trader, you may be familiar with options on individual stocks where you have the right to buy (call option) or the right to sell (put option) a particular stock at some predetermined price within some predetermined time. The buyer has the rights and the seller the obligations. With index options the basic ideas are the same. Index options allow you to make investment decisions on a specific market industry or on the market as a whole. Spread strategies can be made with index options similar to those made with individual stock
