AM "12" OU facts Legendary head coach Bud Wilkinson was 145-29-4 with 3 National Championships at OU. In 1985, heading into the Orange Bowl, Barry Switzer's record was 144-29-4, with 2 National Championships. A win by Switzer would mean the two men would be dead even. He won. Bud Wilkinson was replaced by his defensive coordinator, Gomer Jones, who had a difficult time replacing a legend, and was replaced by Jim Mackenzie.
