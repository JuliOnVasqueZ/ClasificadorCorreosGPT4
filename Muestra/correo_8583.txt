Merger-mania continued in the media and entertainment sectors as Clear Channel Communications (NYSE:CCU), the U.S. radio and billboard giant, and SFX Entertainment (NYSE:SFX), a provider of live entertainment shows, jointly announced that Clear Channel will acquire SFX in a deal valued at approximately $2.7 billion. By expanding Clear Channel's presence in areas such as concerts, sporting events and theatrical productions in more than half of the top 50 U.S. markets, the acquisition is expected to result in greater value for Clear Channel's 1,000 radio stations worldwide.
