San Diego utility customers were hit hard last summer as their local utility emerged from the mandatory rate freeze imposed by the state's 1996 utility deregulation scheme. Davis and the Legislature responded, in effect, by buying some time. They enacted legislation rolling back power bills temporarily, but leaving it unclear whether ratepayers, taxpayers, San Diego Gas and Electric Co. or power suppliers would eventually eat the wholesale costs that led to the spikes.
