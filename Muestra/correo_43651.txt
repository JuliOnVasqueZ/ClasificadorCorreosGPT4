Nelson Attached is the status report and motion for extension of time filed on Friday, December 14 by the Generator Interconnection Coalition drafting committee. This group is the smaller subset of the larger industry-wide plenary group formed as a result of the FERC's directive to convene an Interconnection conference to develop a consensus Interconnection Agreement and Procedures document that all of industry would adopt. As the industry plenary session yielded as much results as could be expected in a large group setting, the drafting committees were formed
