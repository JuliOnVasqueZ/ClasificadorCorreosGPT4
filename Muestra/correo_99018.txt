They were primarily designed to meet temporary needs associated with local power emergencies, not for sustained use. If those interstate lines were upgraded to greater capacity, then we could move toward actually creating a national power grid. An effective national power grid could result in far more competition among producers of electric power across the nation, and far more options to meet demand in any local area.
