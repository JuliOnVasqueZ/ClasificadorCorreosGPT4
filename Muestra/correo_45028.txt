Credit spread movements within the investment grade spectrum are represented in the S&P U.S. Industrial Investment Grade Credit Index while movements within the speculative grade rating spectrum are captured in the S&P U.S. Industrial Speculative Grade Credit Index. Each credit index represents the distribution of U.S. corporate issues across different rating categories and subsectors.
