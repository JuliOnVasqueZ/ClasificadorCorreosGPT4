Moreover, as the recent outbreak of the Nimda computer virus indicates, it will no longer be possible to take instantaneous transfer of information and financial capital for granted. Terrorist attacks also instil fear, raising the risk premia of global connectivity. Suddenly, the brave new world looks a lot less seamless. Basic economics says that a tax on cross-border connections should reduce the flow of such transactions.
