The PSC has logged more than 12,000 complaints about natural gas service since January over such things as pricing, billing inaccuracies, disconnection notices sent in error and demands for payment of hefty overdue balances. That dwarfs the number of complaints the PSC gets about electricity and telecommunications. "People aren't being treated right," says PSC member Stan Wise, a supporter of deregulation who wants to save the system.
