included. DF The National Association of Gas Consumers (an association of municipal gas systems) has filed a complaint against all sellers of natural gas in the US to re-impose wholesale prices caps. They are seeking a benchmark (soft cap) of $2.74/MMBtu. The benchmark figure is based on an average gas forecast from a recent National Petroleum Council study. (Ken Lay is a member of the NPC). The procedural rules are a bit fuzzy. Ordinarily, respondents have 20 days to answer a complaint. However, FERC rules provide for service on respondents.
