Mr. Adams, What is that round black thing on Dogbert's face? My friend says it's his mouth but I say it's his nose. Floyd Dear Flawed, I get this question often, thanks to Mr. Adams' prodigious artistic range. Allow me to clarify some of the shapes in the Dilbert strip. In general, the black circles are dog noses. The black ovals are mouths. Rectangles are desks or sometimes computers. When the ovals are turned the other way and are not filled with black they are glasses. Everything else is random.
