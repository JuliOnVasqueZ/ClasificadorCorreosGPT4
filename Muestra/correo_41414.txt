What is the RTO Rule and why should we care? Last December, the FERC issued Order No. 2000, a final rule on Regional Transmission Organizations (RTO's). Order 2000 builds on the foundation of Orders 888 and 889 (issued in 1996). According to FERC Chairman Jim Hoecker, Order 2000 makes "a persuasive case for separating control of grid operations from the influence of electricity market participants."
