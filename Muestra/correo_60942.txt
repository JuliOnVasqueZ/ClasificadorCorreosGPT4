While the Commission has required certain NERC standards and procedures to be placed in Open Access Transmission Tariffs ("OATT") where the Commission has the power to enforce provisions under the Federal Power Act, in practice the Commission has generally deferred to NERC on transmission reliability questions, including the propriety of TLRs. TLRs inhibit optimal functioning of the transmission system and market because load is not served by the least cost supplier.
