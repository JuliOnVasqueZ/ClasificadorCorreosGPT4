An Eligible Project is any Designated Development Project or Future Project in which an Enron Party holds or aquires an Ownership Interest. A Future Project is any Power Plant or Natural Gas Pipeline (other than the Designated Development Projects) that is located within the Territory and on which construction has commenced or has been completed, provided that pipelines primarily designed to transport liquefied natural gas or natural gas liquids are excluded from Future Projects.
