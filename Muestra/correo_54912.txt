The CAISO monitors electrical loads (i.e., demand) on an on-going basis and ensures that there is an adequate supply of power to meet that demand. 24. The CAISO accomplishes this by maintaining a Real Time Imbalance Market (the "Real Time Market"). When the CAISO receives bids from suppliers that are insufficient to meet the demand for power, it must accept any bid, which then sets the market-clearing price for that hour.
