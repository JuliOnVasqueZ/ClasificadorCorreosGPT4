25, 2001, 10:59PM Enron rating setup irks many workers By L.M. SIXEL TONGUES ARE WAGGING at Enron Corp., and it's not because of the bonuses they're expecting next month. Employees are irate about a new performance-review ranking system that pits them against each other. Under the system, nicknamed "rank and yank," employees will be put in one of five categories: 5 percent will be identified as "superior," 30 percent will be labeled "excellent," 30 percent will be called "strong," 20 percent will be labeled "satisfactory" and 15 percent will be called "needs improvement" or "issues An insider said "needs improvement"
