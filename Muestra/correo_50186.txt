All of the swap agreements involving financial institutions must be registered with CETIP or with another custody and clearing system authorized by the Central Bank or the CVM (Brazilian SEC). As a matter of practice, the agreements used by the banks that participate in the swap market do not follow a standard form, since normally each bank has a particular sample form with which it feels more comfortable.
