(iii) Norway - There is a material risk that the credit product could be regarded as gaming in Norway, in which case trades would be void and unenforceable. The normal way to mitigate this risk in Norway is by using a "substantial professional effort" to satisfy ourselves that a trade is being entered into for valid commercial purposes (normally hedging) by our counterparty. Clearly, it will not be possible to take such steps on a trade by trade basis in the context of EnronOnline.
