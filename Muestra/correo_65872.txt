State Farm Insurance, Michigan's largest provider of homeowner insurance, said it had 441 dog bites claims last year, up nearly 41 percent from 1996. "For the last two years, Michigan has been the fifth-highest state in the amount paid out in dog claims by State Farm," said John Carroll, a company spokesman. The insurer's Michigan costs last year were $81,367 in medical claims and another $3.27 million to settle liability claims. "We don't deny coverage based on breed since any breed can be trained to bite," Carroll said.
