Calpine's 11 QF plants, which generate around 600 megawatts of power from small natural gas-fired and geothermal plants, are part of a fleet of qualifying facilities that can produce up to about a third of all the power generated in California. As of March 31, Pacific Gas and Electric owed a total of $643 million for power the QFs have supplied to its customers, according to the California Public Utilities.
