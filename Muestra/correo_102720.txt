Dabhol and the do nothing Government By Prem Shankar Jha Ever since the Vajpayee's Government came to power more than two and a half years ago, the economy has been sliding relentlessly into the deepest recession it has ever known. In the nine months since December industrial growth has averaged 1.7 per cent. The decade when it averaged 9 per cent and the months, only five years ago, when it touched 16.2 per cent, are now but a distant memory.
