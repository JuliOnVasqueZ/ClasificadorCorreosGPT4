information: Microsoft releases procedure to eliminate "Server-Side Image Map Components" vulnerability A vulnerability in several Web server products from Microsoft could let a malicious Web site visitor perform actions that the system permissions allow, but could not have been accessed without this hole. The risk is in FrontPage 97 and 98 extensions htimage.exe and imagemap.exe, which provide server-side image-mapping support. Microsoft says the affect of this bug is limited, but the company is providing a means of remedying the situation.
