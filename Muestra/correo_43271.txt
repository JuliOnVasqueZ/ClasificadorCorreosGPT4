FERC found MISO's independence satisfactory in most respects, but said that transmission owners cannot be permitted to have veto privileges on filings that affect pricing. FERC conditioned MISO's RTO status on modification of the MISO Agreement to give it the authority to propose rates, terms and conditions of transmission service under the MISO tariff.
