Virus Alerts The Internet is constantly being flooded with information about computer viruses and Trojan Horses. However, within among real virus notices are computer virus hoaxes. While these hoaxes do not infect systems, they are still time consuming and costly to handle. It only wastes bandwidth and un-necessarily alarms other computer users.
