Measuring damages as of a date or dates before the date of liquidation, termination or acceleration will occur in very unusual circumstances. An example was the need to use the most recent available information for the Kuwaiti Dinar after the virtual disappearance of the currency after the invasion of Kuwait in 1990. The non-bankrupt party is not given unlimited discretion to determine the date as of which damages are to be measured.
