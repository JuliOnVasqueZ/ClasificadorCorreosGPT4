Balancing. Monthly balancing on the PG&E system shall be the Seller's responsibility, subject to the limitations of the YCC's natural gas transportation agreement with PG&E, and subject to the PG&E gas system rules and tariffs. Seller shall be responsible for any gas imbalance charges incurred on the PG&E System. Usage Estimates. For the purpose of assisting Seller in the Nomination process, YCC shall make usage estimates in advance and as usage occurs. Seller should specify requested frequency of usage estimates. Monthly True-Up. Actual usage at the Facility will almost certainly be of some variance to the forecasted monthly gas volumes.
