Interruptible gas customers with resid or distillate fuel back-up have already switched to oil, so it is now firm gas supply customers with the potential to add to the already high level of distillate demand. So far in December US distillate demand is running at a record high December level of 3.9 mbd. However, only a small portion of this demand is the result of economically based fuel-switching from gas to distillate.
