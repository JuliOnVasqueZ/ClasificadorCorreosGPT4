In light of the September 11 terrorist attacks, the Bush administration is faced with the unique challenge of building a national consensus for what could be the longest, most complex, and most difficult war in American history. Walter Russell Mead will discuss how America's foreign policy tradition, defense culture, and diplomatic history will influence President Bush and America in responding to the terrorist threat.
