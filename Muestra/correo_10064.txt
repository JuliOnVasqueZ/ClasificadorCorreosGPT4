The bill that resulted addresses the problems caused by irresponsible consumerism, an aggressive credit industry, lax bankruptcy laws and lawyer-run bankruptcy mills. It sets up a flexible formula for bankruptcy judges to channel those with repayment capacity to Chapter 13. Currently, the overwhelming majority of debtors file under Chapter 7, which effectively wipes away their debts.
