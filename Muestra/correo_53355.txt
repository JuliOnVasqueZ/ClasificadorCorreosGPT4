Subsection (a) of this section does not oblige Delaware corporations to indemnify those who serve other enterprises at their request; if a corporation wishes not to extend indemnification rights to those who serve elsewhere at its request, it can say so in its bylaws, or it can say nothing at all, thereby achieving the same result.
