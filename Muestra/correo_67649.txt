Our interviews are conducted at the managers' workstations where our team will spend about 1/2 hour observing the manager in his/her daily work and asking questions. We will then spend the next hour with his/her administrative assistant. Interviewees do not have to prepare anything. We prefer to interview people where they work because often the work environment can give us a lot of insights about what managers need from a software product.
