Teach For America's mission is to have an immediate impact on the lives of thousands of our nation's most under-served students while at the same time building an ever-expanding force of leaders who will work throughout their lives to effect the fundamental changes needed to improve our education system. In New York City, 217 corps members are teaching in Harlem, the South Bronx, and Washington Heights.
