The technological innovations associated with the internet brought about an overload of information which inevitably affects the ways in which consumers make choices. Furthermore, companies have a great deal of influence on how much information consumers can have access to. The problem of pricing substitute products is an important strategic issue faced by internet companies. Consumers usually search for generic products (e.g. VCRs or computers) without knowing exactly what they will buy. Companies can show different products and different prices to each consumer.
