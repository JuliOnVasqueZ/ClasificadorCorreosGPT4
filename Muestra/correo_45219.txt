Van Ness University of Memphis, Memphis, TN 38152, , , USA Marshall University, Huntington, WV 25755, , , USA Abstract We examine the role of limit-order traders and specialists in the market-making process. We find that a large portion of posted bid--ask quotes originates from the limit-order book without direct participation by specialists, and that competition between traders and specialists has a significant impact on the bid--ask spread.
