From Telge and HWY 290, keep going north on 290 for 12 miles. At the 11.5 mile mark you will see a Texaco Station to your right. Keep going past the intersection by the Texaco station and turn right at your next opportunity (i.e. Roberts Road). You will see an "Oakwood Homes" pre-fab housing lot on the corner of 290 and Roberts Road. Turn right at Roberts Road, and go about 8/10 of a mile to Zube park (on your right). Park in the Northwest corner of the parking lot. GROUPS OF 10-15 RIDERS ARE SENT OUT BASED ON RIDE
