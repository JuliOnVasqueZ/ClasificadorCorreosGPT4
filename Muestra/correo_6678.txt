St. Genevieve, Cap Rock and Fall Creek. So-called boutique wines from California or from small Texas wineries almost never make it to the grocery shelves. Current law says Texans cannot bring into the state more than 3 gallons (about two cases) of wine per month. And buyers must accompany the wine, instead of shipping it. There are similar limits on beer and liquor. Also, Texas wineries can sell only 25,000 gallons a year to customers at a winery if it is in a "wet" precinct that allows the sale of alcohol.
