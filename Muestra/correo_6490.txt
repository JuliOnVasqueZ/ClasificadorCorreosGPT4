The confirmation letters (for all financial power trades) that I send out have the following payment date language on them: The fifth (5th) Business Day following the date on which the Floating Price is determinable (for trades one month or longer) The fifth (5th) Business Day following the last day of the month that includes the Determination Period (for trades less than one month) The language on EnronOnline is different.
