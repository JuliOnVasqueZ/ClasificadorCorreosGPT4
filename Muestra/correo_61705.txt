While gas-fired plants are much cheaper and quicker to build than traditional coal-fired plants, many industry observers doubt that U.S. gas supply can grow to meet future demand, expected to reach 30 trillion cubic feet or more per year by 2020. "Coal will not go away," said Steve Bergstrom, president of Dynegy Inc. (DYN). "The coal industry will figure it out. There are too many jobs involved and it's too cheap."
