9:30-11:30am Media Briefing President Clinton will visit South Asia from March 20-26th this year, the first visit by a U.S. president to the region in 22 years. He will visit Bangladesh and perhaps Pakistan, but will spend the bulk of his time in India, reflecting the primary objective of this visit- to develop a broader, more multi-faceted relationship between the world's oldest and the world's largest democracies.
