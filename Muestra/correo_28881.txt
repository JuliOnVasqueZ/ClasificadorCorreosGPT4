Enron Corp. Enron is the energy industry's largest marketer/trader/risk manager and one of the largest natural gas pipeline operators. About 60% of its marketing and trading business today is done over the EnronOnline system. Critics charged that EnronOnline provides a tremendous advantage to its parent because all of the transaction data is thrown into a historical database that is available only to Enron. EnronOnline has handled more than 280,000 transactions worth $140 billion since going live.
