PARA Folha de S.Paulo, 29 Feb 2000, p. 3-8:- The Brazilian subsidiary of the American oil company Texaco will keep cleaning the river Para probably by the first weekend of March 2000. The company already removed 25% of the total 1.9 tonnes of fuel BPF spilled in the river. ARGENTINA: LOWERING TAXES, IMPOSSIBLE FOR NOW El Cronista Comercial, 29 Feb 2000, p.2:- Officials at the Argentine Revenue Service warn that it would be impossible to lower taxes for now. Many are requesting that fuel and value added taxes be lowered but this would mean US$ 2bn less in government revenues.
