In a release, Fenosa said the deal involves the construction of a combined-cycle power plant with a 500MW potency, which will use natural gas from a regasification plant on the island nation. The 500MW potency is equivalent to 30% of the Caribbean island's electricity demand. The deal also includes a complimentary project, Fenosa said. This is the construction of a regasification plant on the republic's coastline, with a capacity for treating gas equivalent to the consumption of various groups of combined-cycle plants with a potency of 1300MW.
