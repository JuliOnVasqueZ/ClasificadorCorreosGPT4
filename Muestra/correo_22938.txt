voicemail. Ed, Residential Obligation Section 39.352(g) of Senate Bill 7 provides that for any REP serving an aggregate load in excess of 300 MW, 5% of the load must consist of residential customers. A REP can fulfil this requirement by: 1. Demonstrating that it directly serves residential load amounting to 5% of its total load 2. That another REP serves the residential load on its behalf, or 3. It pays 1 dollar per MWH of required 5% load into the System Benefit Fund. EES can fulfil its obligation by entering into an agreement with New Power to serve residential load
