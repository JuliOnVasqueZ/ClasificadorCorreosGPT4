Spreadsheet simulation can be used to determine the probability distribution of the payout of the contract and the probability distribution for the net operating contribution including the contract payout, which is the basis for comparing alternatives. It is instructive for the students to think about what would be the ideal contract to reduce exposure to the greatest intent. This pushes students towards the optimal hedge ratio.
