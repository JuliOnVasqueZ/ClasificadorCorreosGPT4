The new contract also has two other firm products. "Firm (Liquidated Damages)" is closest to the WSPP firm product, but it defines force majeure more strictly than WSPP. "Firm (LD-System Reliability)" explicitly allows a selling utility to curtail deliveries without paying liquidated damages if necessary to maintain native load. Of particular interest to traders of eastern U.S. electricity is the fact that the EEI contract defines what an "into" product is.
