Harbor is a classic beach bar/rest. - lots of fun and good food & drinks Sandy Cay is everyone's image of a desert island and perfect for a lunch stop and a swim; don't miss Cane Garden Bay (unless the northerly swell is too heavy) there's a wonderful swath of pure white beach with palm trees swaying overhead and steel drum bands serenade you into the evening - watch the reefs but the channel in is well marked; if there's a full moon be sure to be on Tortola somewhere that night (Cane Garden works fine) to get to Bomba's
