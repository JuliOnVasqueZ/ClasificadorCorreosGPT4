Customers who pick the last option get ComEd's market rate but are allowed to choose ComEd competitors as their primary power suppliers. ComEd's proposal would bar its largest customers from that option, which the utility says has stymied deregulation because it allows competing suppliers to merely resell the power that ComEd produced. "So long as the utility is providing this option, real competitive markets won't work," said Arlene Juracek, ComEd's vice president for regulatory and strategic services.
