Monday, Duke suspended a case it brought against the California Independent System Operator, which manages 75 percent of the state's power grid. Duke and other power generators sued the ISO after the water resources department said it would not pay for power bought on the expensive spot market. The ISO had said it would bill the nearly bankrupt utilities for that power. When power generators tried to pull out of the spot market, the ISO sued and a federal judge ordered companies to keep selling to the state.
