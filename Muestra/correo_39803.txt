However, the utility may alter, refuse or discontinue service if neither the trustee nor the debtor, within 20 days after the date of the order for relief, furnished adequate assurance of payment for service after such date. I'm no bankruptcy expert, but my point is simply that our rights may change once a bankruptcy action is filed. The Commission has held that termination of service cannot be inconsistent with federal bankruptcy law: see El Paso Natural Gas Company, 61 FERC P61,302
