If approved, the two rate hikes together will increase transmission and distribution costs by about 50% for business customers that leave the utility for competing power suppliers. Perhaps more important, the new rates would clear the way for delivery charge increases of the same magnitude on all Chicago-area power users in three years, when a freeze on ComEd's rates expires. Transmission and distribution rates account for 20% to 25% of a typical business customer's total power bill - so, a typical bill could rise as much as 10% with these increases.
