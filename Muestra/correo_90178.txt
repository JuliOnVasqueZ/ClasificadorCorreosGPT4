Russian exports of domestic oil production (excluding transit volumes) reached 2.5 mbd in November, after remaining fairly steady at about 2.35 mbd from July though October. Fourth quarter oil exports are expected to average 2.5 mbd, 0.3 mbd greater than in the fourth quarter 1999. CERA estimates Iraqi oil production averaged 2.91 mbd in November-down 0.1 mbd from the October level.
