But it consumes less on a per-capita basis than all other states except Rhode Island. The mild climate and less heavy industry than other states are part of the reason. Experts say credit also must go to stringent conservation measures. ``It's true we're big, and it's true we didn't build a lot of power plants. ... But we're not energy hogs at all,'' said Arthur Rosenfeld, who sits on the five-member California Energy Commission.
