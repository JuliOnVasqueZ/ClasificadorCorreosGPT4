Tie-in assemblies will be required on both the Mainline and Loopline at stations 1, 2, and 3. New block and side valve assemblies for new compressor and scrubbers will be installed on the suction side main and loop lines at each station. The tie-in will entail blocking (isolating) a line segment within the station yard on the suction side (each line done separately) thus creating a dead leg back to the next upstream block valve crossover(s).
