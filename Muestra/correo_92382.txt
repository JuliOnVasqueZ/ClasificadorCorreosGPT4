Houston's Future recently committed resoundingly to the creation of Goals and a Strategic Plan for Houston that will allow our community to achieve its extraordinary potential over the next decisive 20 years. This landmark program, already underway, will be assisted by many dedicated volunteers from throughout the community. Led by Chairman Gene Vaughan, the Center, an affiliate of the Greater Houston Partnership, is focused on very tough questions: The world and Houston are changing rapidly - what will be the new reality in 20 years and beyond? What do we want Houston to be? What are the obstacles and trade-offs?
