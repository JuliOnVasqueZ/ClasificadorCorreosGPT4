Part of the explanation for March's record power-buying binge was City Light's recent decision to store up extra water behind Ross Dam on the Skagit River instead of spilling it through the dam's power turbines. In a normal water year, City Light officials would be spilling water for energy and expecting it would be replaced with spring runoff.
