Section 216(e)(2) provides for notice to FERC of penalties imposed by an electric reliability organization, and authorizes FERC to affirm, set aside or modify any penalty imposed by an electric reliability organization. Section 216(e)(3) authorizes FERC to assign enforcement of reliability standards to regional transmission organizations.
