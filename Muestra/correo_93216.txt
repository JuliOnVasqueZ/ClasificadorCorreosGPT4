Services are unique, and there are many cases where customers will only buy one of many services offered by a given company. Our model will help companies decide which services to offer to which customers and how much to charge for these services. Research Strategy: Our research strategy is to divide the pricing problem into two components which can be combined to generate optimal pricing strategies. These components are choice models and optimization models.
