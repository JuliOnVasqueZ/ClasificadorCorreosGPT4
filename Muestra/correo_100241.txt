The city of Roseville, near Sacramento, entered into a $114 million contract with Enron last month to purchase 50 megawatts of electricity for five years at $49 per megawatt-hour. Furthermore, the city has started talks with Enron about building and operating a 750-megawatt natural gas-fired power plant that city officials expect will contribute to lower costs for the purchase of electricity. Mayor Harry Crabb said the plant could protect residents from the price spikes that have plagued customers of SDG&E since June.
