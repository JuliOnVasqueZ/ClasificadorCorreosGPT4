Consumer rates were frozen and customers were given the option to buy electricity from nonutility retail suppliers. But tight electricity supplies and a flawed auction system led to a sharp rise in wholesale power costs. With retail rates frozen, Pacific Gas and Edison racked up multibillion-dollar deficits. In January, Mr. Davis declared an emergency and put the state into the power-buying business. Since then, he hasn't been bashful about exercising his emergency powers.
