The PUC directs the utilities to calculate their cost of capital-related revenue requirements using the amounts in their respective Transition Cost Balancing Accounts (TCBA) as the rate base. The TCBA reflects the net book value of utility generation assets less the recovery of those assets* stranded costs. The PUC will allow utilities to record in a memorandum account the difference between this reduced rate of return component of revenue requirements and their last PUC authorized rate of return for the same assets, for possible future use in adjusting the TCBA-based rates.
