The increased liquidity will be available to suppliers and users to manage price risk, reduce financial uncertainty and bring greater economic performance into the Alberta electricity market. Watt-Ex is an independently owned and operated online trading system for electrical energy in the Alberta market. It allows market participants to buy and sell contracts for firm forward commitments in the real time or spot electricity market, as well as providing for the trade of ancillary services.
