New York experience "We wrestled with this: Should government really be in this business?" says David Fountaine, village administrator in Hamburg, N.Y., a suburb of Buffalo that formed a buying group for its residents in April 1999. "In our case, by being in it, we've helped our residents." Hamburg and nearby Sloan, N.Y., together hired a unit of Texaco Inc. to supply gas to residents who signed up for the communities' program.
