To help solve the state's supply shortfall, DENA said it remained committed to adding substantial generation in California. Within two months and upon receiving final regulatory approvals, the company will begin construction on 1,000 megawatts at its Moss Landing facility. The expansion of the current 1,478-megawatt facility will be in commercial operation by summer 2002.
