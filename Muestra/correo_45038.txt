Current Assets This item primarily represents cash, inventories, accounts receivables, marketable securities, and other current assets. Total Assets Total Assets and every other variable are entered in thousands of dollars. For example, $15,500,000 should be entered as 15500. Specifically, total assets are the sum of current assets plus net property, plant, and equipment plus other noncurrent assets (including intangible assets, deferred items, and investments and advances).
