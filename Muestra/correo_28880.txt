Altrade operates its own independent online exchange that is one of Enron's biggest electronic competitors. It differs from EnonOnline in that various buyers and sellers trade with each other with Altrade serving to clear the transactions. On EnronOnline, Enron posts bid and asked prices and is a party in every transaction.
