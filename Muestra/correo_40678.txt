Receiving cash from shippers in accordance with the Tariff's fuel charge does not reduce the consumption of natural gas volumes in rendering transportation service, Instead it provides economic assets (cash) to the company to pay for the gas volumes that have to be acquired to run the compressors. Therefore the proceeds from the fuel charge represent transportation revenues for the company, not a fuel or gas purchase expense reduction.
