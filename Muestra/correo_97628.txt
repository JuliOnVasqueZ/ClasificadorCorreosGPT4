Now on with the latest patches and alerts: Panda Software warns of W95/CIH virus W95/CIH (or Chernobyl) is set to go active on April 26, the anniversary of the Chernobyl nuclear disaster of 1986. The virus infects various executables with 1K byte of code, and on the 26th is set to destroy the Flash BIOS of Intel Pentium processors, rendering the computer useless until the Flash BIOS is restored to its original settings.
