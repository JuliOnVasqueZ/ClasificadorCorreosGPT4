Prices are on an upward trend for 1999 and 2000, the result of both higher regional spot natural gas prices and the increasing value of ``pure capacity'' -- the value of ensuring reliability for consumers. The study also evaluates the long-term future of the Western market and highlights the following key results: Capacity Requirements Growing Steadily. The region's underlying demand growth is strong, creating a long-term regional capacity need totaling more than 18,000 MW by 2005, unless there is a serious economic downturn in the region.
