Bilateral Transactions Under the Act, bilateral transactions in all commodities (other than agriculture commodities) that do not occur on trading facility (i.e. not on a multi-party exchange) are exempt from most provisions of the CEA as long as the transactions are entered solely between "eligible contract participants"(i.e. persons and certain legal entities satisfying capital thresholds and other requirements under the Act).
