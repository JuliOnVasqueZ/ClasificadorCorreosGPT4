how the credit system uses RisktRAC differently from market risk Market risk picks up all officialized books. If a book is not officialized it does not pick the book up. Credit picks up all officalized books. If a book is not officialized, it picks up the previous day's position. Therefore, if positions in book A are moved to book B, and A is no longer officialized, credit picks up the previous day's position for book A.
