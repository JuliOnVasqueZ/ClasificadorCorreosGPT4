To consumers and industrial users in California, the effects of a national electricity transmission grid would be real and enduring, but not spectacular. It would, however, go far to achieving the quiet result that every consumer seeks - lower prices, more reliable electric power supplies. There is no single solution to California's energy crisis, but developing a national interstate electricity highway system would go far toward addressing it.
