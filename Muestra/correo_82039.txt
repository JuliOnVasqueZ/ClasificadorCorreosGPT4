Section 6.2. In response to the concerns over backing by a creditworthy party, the ISO approached the California Department of Water Resources (CDWR/CERS) to provide this financial backing. CDWR/CERS has concluded that it will not provide backing for the program as it is now designed. Discussions continue with CDWR/CERS and it appears that backing may be provided, albeit for a modified program design. The ISO will publish details of such modified program when they become available.
