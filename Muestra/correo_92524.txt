Today, from the outside, Stiglitz is increasing his criticism of the U.S. Treasury/IMF policy toward Asia, Latin America and Russia, which is still fundamentally unchanged despite the great scare of 1997-98. Stiglitz himself may not join the expected protests this week in Washington at the IMF/World Bank annual meetings.
