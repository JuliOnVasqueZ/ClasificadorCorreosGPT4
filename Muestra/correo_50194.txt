CC 5 Account A possible alternative way to structure a prepay would be by using a non-registered loan with the Central Bank. Under such alternative, the funds disbursed by Enron under the loan would be remitted to Brazil through an international transfer of Brazilian currency, as allowed by Circular 2677 of the Central Bank of Brazil.
