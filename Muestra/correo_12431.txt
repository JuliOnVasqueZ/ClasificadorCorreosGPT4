While perhaps faster and easier to use than phone calls, faxes, or letters, an Internet-based platform is just another medium for trade, and speed and ease of use do not dictate that the Commission's affiliate abuse concerns have been satisfied. There is, for example, no assurance that the posted prices on EnronOnline 9 represent prevailing market prices.
