In 1864 he became a resident master in Elgin's Weston House Academy, where he conducted his first studies in sound. Appropriately, Bell had begun professionally as he would continue through life--as a teacher-scientist. In 1868 he became his father's assistant in London and assumed full charge while the senior Bell lectured in America.
