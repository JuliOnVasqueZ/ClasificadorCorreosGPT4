OF THE GOVERNOR PR01: 285 FOR IMMEDIATE RELEASE 06/10/2001 GOVERNOR DAVIS ORDERS FINANCIAL INCENTIVES FOR BUSINESSES WHO CUT ENERGY USE Order Calls for Streamlining of Energy Demand Reduction Plans LOS ANGELES Governor Gray Davis today signed Executive Order D-39-01, which authorizes the California Department of Water Resources (DWR) to implement voluntary, emergency load curtailment programs for commercial, industrial or other large businesses. In exchange for agreeing to reduce electricity usage during power emergencies, the DWR will offer financial incentives to participating customers.
