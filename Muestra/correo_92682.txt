The only way to control the risks in the catastrophe options is to have a diversified portfolio of other contracts, and none of the exchanges currently offers a sufficiently broad range of options to provide for that hedge, he said. Florida windstorm options, for example, cannot be bought and then hedged in the same way that International Business Machines Corp. stock options contracts can be hedged with IBM stock, Mr. Lane explained.
