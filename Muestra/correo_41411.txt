"In the open access final rule (Order No. 888), the Commission issues a single pro forma tariff describing the minimum terms and conditions of service to bring about this nondiscriminatory open access transmission service. All public utilities that own, control, or operate interstate transmission facilities are required to offer service to others under the pro forma tariff. They must also use the pro forma tariffs for their own wholesale energy sales and purchases. Order No. 888 also provides for the full recovery of stranded costs--that is, costs that were prudently incurred to serve power customers
