The rates for the standard service package will be a market rate, unless the incumbent utility chooses to offer a regulated rate approved by the Arkansas Public Service Commission. The proposal advanced by the distribution cooperatives would enable them to create a joint venture to market electric power to retail customers throughout Arkansas. All retail marketing activities outside the traditional local territories of the distribution cooperatives would be conducted through the joint marketing venture.
