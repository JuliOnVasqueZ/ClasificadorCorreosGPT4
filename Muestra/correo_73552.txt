Amgen's products include the billion-dollar blockbusters Epogen (EPO) and Neupogen, as well as Infergen. Epogen is used to treat anemia in patients with kidney failure or patients undergoing chemotherapy. Neupogen is used to stimulate the immune system and Infergen treats chronic hepatitis. Many other drugs are in the pipeline, but the most important new drug is NESP--a next-generation drug to succeed EPO.
