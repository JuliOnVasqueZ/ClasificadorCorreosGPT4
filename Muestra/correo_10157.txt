In Argentina, Brazil and Chile, where power comes mainly from hydroelectric dams, the industry is vulnerable to fluctuations in rainfall. Meanwhile, privatization and deregulation of the electricity market in these countries will require generators and distributors to manage shortfalls and excesses in power more efficiently, a function financial markets are already fulfilling in the US and Europe. The use of financial hedges to mitigate risk is a trend companies and regulators in Latin America want to encourage.
