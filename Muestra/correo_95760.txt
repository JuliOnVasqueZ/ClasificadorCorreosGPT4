A number of the antivirus vendors have issued alerts about the Liberty virus that allegedly infects PalmOS machines. The Trojan is said to come disguised as an update to a legitimate Palm application, Gambit Studios LLC's Liberty GameBoy emulation software. However, when executed, the program attempts to delete all applications on the Palm. Computer Associates, Symantec and Trend Micro all say they're the first to release protection for the virus.
