Swaps (Pulp & Paper, Petrochem, Power) OTC Swaps The Central Bank regulates swap transactions involving local financial institutions acting either as dealers for their own account or as intermediaries to the contracting parties. When the financial institutions act as intermediaries, the agreements are in fact private agreements negotiated by two non-financial companies, but become subject to the requirements and regulations normally applicable to swaps involving financial institutions.
