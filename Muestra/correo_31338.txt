In the current option manager, the only possibility is to increase or decrease the ATM suite vol (which has little effect on the wing strikes anyway). For that reason we need a volumetric control for each strike. The volumeteric control could be a toggle which could be turned on or off. If the volumetric control toggle si on, corresponding to each strike, there would be a maximum volume. If the strike is hit or lifted by that volume, that strike is automatically suspended and the trader notified.
