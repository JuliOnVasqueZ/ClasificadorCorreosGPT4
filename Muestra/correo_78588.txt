For example, the group that handles U.S. gas settlements is not the same group as the one handling Australian Power. The transactions are transmitted to the appropriate back office utilizing a "bridge": a process that is capable of communicating transaction information into the variety of systems that Enron operates. The potential for manual input error is thereby removed providing Enron and the customer with more efficient recording of the transaction. The back office systems at Enron
