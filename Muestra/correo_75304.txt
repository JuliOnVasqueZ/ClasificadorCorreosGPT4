Columbia can match the highest bid, subject to a present value process, and the sale is immediate and binding. At any time before a binding sale, the customer can change or withdraw its bid. Columbia's ``bid/offer'' auction proposal meets the six basic principles outlined in FERC's Order 637. * Columbia's auction has predictable timing since the auction will begin each day at 9:15 a.m. (EST) and all available capacity will be posted prior to 8:00 a.m. (EST). *
