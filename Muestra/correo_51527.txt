Used to Determine Need for Tap Filter/Separators. Originally 29 locations were identified to receive the small filter separators. These locations were considered Priority 1 sites. The determination of a Priority 1 site is based upon the following criteria: 1) a location where liquids were collected twice and 2) ppm concentration of the liquids collected was > 1.0 ppm. Based upon these criteria, Ridgecrest and Bakersfield (Kern River Station) have been added to the list of Priority sites. Flow through at the Kern River location is 300-500 mmscf/day.
