Best, Jeff August 22, 2001 Page One Feature For Montana Power, a Broadband Dream May Turn Out to Be More of a Nightmare By BILL RICHARDS Special to THE WALL STREET JOURNAL BUTTE, Mont. -- This is a gamblers' town. More than a century ago, Marcus Daly turned a bet on a copper vein under Butte into the giant Anaconda Co. The gambling game Keno was invented here for bored miners. In 1974, stuntman Evel Knievel, a Butte native, made headlines betting -- unsuccessfully -- that he could jump the Snake River
