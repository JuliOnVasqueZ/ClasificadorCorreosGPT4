To join the Houston Society, you also have to be a member of the national AIMR organization. For AIMR membership, go to and fill out the Membership Application Form. The HSFA currently requires you to have three sponsors: your supervisor and two HSFA members, one of whom must be outside your firm. (If your supervisor is an HSFA member, then only two sponsors are required.) If you do not know any HSFA members, there are people who have volunteered to serve as sponsors.
