Information and Arts Minister Lee Yock Suan said he was set to introduce a bill that would allow the government to restrict distribution of a broadcast service if the company was deemed to be meddling in the city-state's internal affairs. Singapore's Newspaper Act can ban distribution of a newspaper or magazine and has been used against The Economist and Asian Wall Street Journal, but up until now broadcasters have been left alone.
