Shares of Amazon.com (Nasdaq:AMZN) and drugstore.com (Nasdaq:DSCM) stood out in the Internet sector today after the leading online retailer and online drugstore announced an expansion of their business relationship. Under terms of the deal, drugstore.com will be given a shopping "tab" on Amazon.com's site in exchange for $105 million over 3 years. In addition, Amazon.com will invest another $30 million in drugstore.com, which raises its stake in the online drugstore to roughly 28%.
