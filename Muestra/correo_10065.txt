The measure also seeks to improve the bankruptcy system for consumers by promoting credit counseling services and educational courses for debtors prior to filing a bankruptcy petition. The plan strengthens enforcement and penalties against coercive tactics by creditors. It penalizes creditors who seek a double-payment and establishes tough penalties for creditors who use threats to coerce debtors into voluntarily agreeing to pay a debt which could be wiped away in bankruptcy.
