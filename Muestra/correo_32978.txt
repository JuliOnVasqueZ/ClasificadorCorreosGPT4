Under a contract path transmission model, control areas do not have to keep day-ahead balanced schedules and can "play" in the energy market with unbalanced portfolios up until the schedule ramping times. Non-control areas can only get transactions approved for scheduling under specific source and sink requirements that forces them to submit balanced interchange schedules. This is a major reason why competitive generators have sought to become NERC certified control areas.
