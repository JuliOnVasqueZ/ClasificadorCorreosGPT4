Power, a Canadian coal buyer, claims it is entitled to a refund of unconstitutionally imposed excise tax on coal it bought from several US producers. The IRS is apparently acknowledging that the coal excise tax is unconstitutional as applied to exported coal, and has announced that it will refund taxes improperly levied. But at issue is whether coal buyers are properly the ones who are due the refund, or whether it more properly belongs to the coal producers.
