Square, where folks queue up mid-morning for half-priced same-day tickets to some great shows...and some real dogs. You won't find any Producers tickets there, unfortunately, but Rent may be available. See Phantom of the Opera and Les Miserables, for sure. Grab lunch at the Carnegie Deli, but don't fall on the floor when you order the $18 reuben sandwich. Pay the $3 split fee and share it with Teri. Note the huge desserts and the terse wait staff.
