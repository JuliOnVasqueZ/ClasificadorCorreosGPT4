Second, modernity has led us to be preoccupied with factuality - with scientifically verifiable and historically reliable facts. Modern Western culture is the only culture in human history that has identified truth with factuality. We are, as Marcus Borg calls us, "fact fundamentalists." If a statement isn't scientifically or historically factual, it isn't true. These two things are serious mistakes and deadly to the journey inward. Let me point out that both liberals and conservatives can be fact fundamentalists.
