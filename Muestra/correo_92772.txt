Of particular interest in the power industry are investments centered around opportunities arising from restructuring of electricity and natural gas sectors as regulation is removed. A large proportion of the risk is embedded in ongoing changes of public policy on an international basis. As an intentionally screwed-up example, can anyone other than a financial genius correctly asses volatility for U.S. companies investing in seed projects in Mexico based on speculation of the inevitable dismantling of the national utility (CFE) and PEMEX?
