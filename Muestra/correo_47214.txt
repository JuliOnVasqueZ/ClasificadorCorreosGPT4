Following the end of the 15-minute Temporary Trading Halt, the market shall reopen in all contract months of this futures contract. When trading resumes, price fluctuation limits for each contract month, except as otherwise provided in this rule, shall be expanded to $2.00 per MMBtu above and below the previous day's settlement price for such contract month.
