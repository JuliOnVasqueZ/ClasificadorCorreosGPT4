The Dabhol Power Company (DPC), 65 percent owned by the Enron Corp, is currently locked in a bitter dispute with a local utility over a payments default. "We want out," said Enron's Chairman Kenneth Lay in an interview with the Financial Times. DPC, which is India's biggest direct foreign investment, had signed a contract to supply power to a state-owned utility, the Maharashtra State Electricity Board (MSEB), from its gas-based plant on the state's west coast. But MSEB stopped buying power in May calling it expensive and defaulted on payments
