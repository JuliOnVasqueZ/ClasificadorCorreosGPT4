Fares are subject to U.S. departure, agricultural, immigrations, customs and security taxes/surcharges of up to USD 47. For travel to some countries, additional airport, transportation, embarkation, security, duty, equalization, passenger service and noise taxes/surcharges of up to USD 35 will apply depending on the destination and may vary slightly depending on currency exchange rates at the time of purchase. For return travel from some countries, fares do not include airport and/or departure taxes of up to USD 37 which may be collected by the foreign government
