Feb 2000, p. 1-14:- The Brazilian National Nuclear Energy Commission (Cnen) established the deadline of March 15th 2000 to release an authorisation for the operation of the nuclear power station Angra 2 of the Brazilian state-owned nuclear electricity company Eletronuclear in the Brazilian city of Angra dos Reis (state of Rio de Janeiro). With the authorisation, Angra 2 can begin to charge its reactor, testing and inspecting safety after that.
