Evidence of such behavior may be derived from a number of circumstances, including: MMIP 2.1.1.1 withholding of generation capacity under circumstances in which it would normally be offered in a competitive market; MMIP 2.1.1.2 unexplained or unusual redeclarations of availability by Generators; MMIP 2.1.1.3 unusual trades or transactions; MMIP 2.1.1.4 pricing and bidding patterns that are inconsistent with prevailing supply and demand conditions, e.g., prices and bids that appear consistently excessive for or otherwise inconsistent with such conditions; and MMIP 2.1.1.5 unusual activity or circumstances relating to imports from or exports to other markets or exchanges.
