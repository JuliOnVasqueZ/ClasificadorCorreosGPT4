Furthermore, in an esoteric concept known as the "Winner's Curse," a bidder realizes that if his bid actually prevails, if the incumbent fails to match it, he almost certainly overbid. Given this, most competitors will not even bother to bid in an auction when an incumbent has the right of first refusal, and those that submit a bid do not rationally invest the time, expense and expertise necessary; they may just "fire off" a "low-ball" bid.
