Legislation on digital signatures varies from state to state. For example, Connecticut law covers only medical records at hospitals, while Illinois makes electronic signatures applicable to all communications. In 1995 California became one of the early adopters of digital signature legislation, but that legislation was limited to communication with government agencies.
