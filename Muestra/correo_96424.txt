Regional Markets--The Topock Premium Buoyed by strong regional power demand and high utilization rates on pipelines into California, Topock continues to be the highest pricing point in North America. High power loads in the West are supporting other prices in the region, but supply growth and pipeline capacity constraints out of the Rockies and high pipeline utilization rates between the San Juan Basin and California have maintained extremely wide differentials between those markets and California.
