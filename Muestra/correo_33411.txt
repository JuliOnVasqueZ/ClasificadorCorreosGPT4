RTO is a transco, it is appropriate for the MM to review whether the RTO is doing something potentially problematic in planning, ATC calcs, etc. MM should only have the ability to recommend changes to market structure, possible penalties, etc. MM should not have the ability to issue a penalty to any one without first going to FERC. It is possible that the MM could ask for FERC pre-approval to issue "penalties" or remedies for certain conduct. The example that comes to mind is the $1000 price cap in PJM.
