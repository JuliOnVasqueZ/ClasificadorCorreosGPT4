NEW YORK, March 7 (Reuters) - America's largest exporters are launching an aggressive lobbying campaign on Capitol Hill to protect the Export-Import Bank from budget reductions, the Wall Street Journal reported in its online edition on Wednesday. The exporters, including Boeing Co. , Caterpillar Inc. , Enron Corp Halliburton Co. and others, are angry and confused that a Republican administration has targeted one of their favourite programmes for big cuts, the paper reported.
