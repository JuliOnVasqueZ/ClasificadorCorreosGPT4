Houston, Oct. 25 (Bloomberg) -- Enron Corp. prided itself on sharing the wealth with employees when the company was posting record profits last year. Now, from the boardroom to the mailroom, employees are sharing the pain. Enron, the largest energy trader, grants stock options to the bulk of its 20,000 employees. Under Enron's standard compensation package, most employees get stock options equal to 5 percent of their annual base salary, spokeswoman Meredith Philipp said.
