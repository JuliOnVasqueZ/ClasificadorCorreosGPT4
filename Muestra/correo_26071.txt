Monday, October 1, 2001 El Paso Merchant Energy (currently EPPS) will begin scheduling energy trades and bids under the new SCID; EPME. The OLD SCID 'EPPS' WILL NO LONGER BE VALID. All of El Paso Merchant Energy's scheduling transactions will use the new SCID beginning with submission of Day Ahead schedules for Trade Date, Monday, October 1, 2001. Any advance schedules that may have already been submitted using the old SCID, 'EPPS', must be removed (deleted) and rescheduled using the new SCID, EPME.
