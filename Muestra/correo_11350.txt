"Transferable Letter of Credit": ECT's standard is to have "transferable" L/C's in order to be consistent with the "transfer" language included in our Master Agreements. The rationale is that if ECT wanted to transfer its rights/obligations under the Agreement to an Affiliate, we would want the L/C to be transferable as well. Put another way - having a non-transferable L/C would be at cross purposes with having a transferable Agreement.
