In fact, I would expect the CFTC to treat power as having a finite supply at any given time. This means, for example, that power derivatives traded on a multilateral transaction execution facility would not qualify for exemption from the Commodity Exchange Act under the current proposal. I think the EEI and other industry groups should consider attempting to persuade the CFTC to exempt physical and derivative power contracts from certain provisions of the CEA under the CFTC's proposal to consider exemptions on a "case-by-case basis."
