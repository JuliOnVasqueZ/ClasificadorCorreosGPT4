On August 23, 2000, President Clinton responded to Governor Davis' request by releasing $2.6 million in emergency funds to help low-income Southern Californians cope with the surge in their electricity bills. The President also asked federal regulators to speed up their investigation into the operation of U.S. power markets and urged the Small Business Administration to use its credit programs to help small firms hurt by the price increases.
