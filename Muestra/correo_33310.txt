RESs whose energy imbalances exceed 25% of their own individual schedules will pay an adder or be charged a discount of 25% of OPC. Adjustments are made to avoid double-counting of adders and discounts applicable to those RESs that are also subject to an adder or discount below. These imbalance charges will be phased-in as follows: * * From October 1, 1999 through December 31, 1999 -- No adder will apply under this provision. * * From January 1, 2000 through March 31, 2000 -- 25% adder/discount will apply to those RESs whose imbalances exceed 25% of their schedules 20%
