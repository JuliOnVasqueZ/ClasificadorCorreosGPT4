They stay with the fallen goose until it dies or is able to fly again. Then, they launch out on their own, or with another formation to catch up with their flock. LESSON: If we have the sense of a goose, we will stand by our colleagues and each other in difficult times as well as in good! ~ Written by Angeles Arrien ~ Message:
