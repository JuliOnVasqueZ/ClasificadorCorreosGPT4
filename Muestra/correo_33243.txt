ABX2 30 (Dutra) This bill would amend CPUC act to authorize a public entity to assume from a public utility the work of relocating utilities that relates to a project of the public entity if the public utility defaults on executing utility relocation work under a relocation agreement with the public entity in connection with a transit or transportation capital improvement project, and would authorize the public entity to issue contracts with another qualified entity
