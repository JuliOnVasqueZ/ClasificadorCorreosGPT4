CATEX's roughly 160 subscribers include reinsurers, insurers and corporate entities that purchase coverage through their captives, Mr. Sweeney said. "We obviously have a long way to go, but we are pretty satisfied with what we have achieved so far," Mr. Sweeney said. The exchange sees increased activity after major losses, as cedents seek to buy replacement coverage to offset depletions in their existing cover, he said.
