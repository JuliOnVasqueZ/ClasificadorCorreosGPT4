The small generators support the bill because it will give them a stable price for five years, avoiding ups and downs and the possibility that state regulators might make a more unfavorable price cut. Much of the current price formula is based on the price of natural gas at the California border, which has soared this winter. The legislation spreads the price bench mark over a five-year period.
