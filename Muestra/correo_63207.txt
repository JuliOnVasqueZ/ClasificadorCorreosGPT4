(New York) Enron Draws Down $3 Bln in Credit to Boost Investor Confidence Houston, Oct. 26 (Bloomberg) -- Enron Corp., whose stock has fallen on concern about the largest energy trader's transactions with affiliates, drew down $3 billion in credit to restore confidence in its financial strength. The Houston-based company will use about $2.2 billion to pay off commercial paper obligations and keep the rest as cash, spokesman Mark Palmer said. ``Nothing instils confidence like cash,'' he said.
