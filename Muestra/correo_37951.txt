FERC decisional precedent indicates that a QF can lawfully sell at market-based rates without seeking market-based rate authorization from FERC, but that it may nevertheless be prudent for a QF to seek FERC acceptance of a market-based rate schedule that would provide protection in the event QF status is lost.
