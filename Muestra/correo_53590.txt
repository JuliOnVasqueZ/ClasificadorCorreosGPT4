Amsterdam is a modern international city, at the crossroads of Europe and at the heart of the Dutch experiment in social freedom. It is also the historic city of Rembrandt, van Gogh, and the Anne Frank House. It is not only the center of Dutch culture but also a melting pot of diversity. The distinctive architecture and numerous canals make Amsterdam one of the world's most unique cities. Easily covered on foot, Amsterdam abounds
