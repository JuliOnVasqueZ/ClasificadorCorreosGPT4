In the initial environmental assessment and study, Transwestern conducted baseline surveys of the area to determine existing noise levels This study was completed to assist Transwestern in the mitigation measures which will be implemented after construction of the Gallup Compresso Station. Pending completion of the facility, Transwestern will conduct post noise studys to determine whether facility noise levels have increased above those levels mandated by FERC.
