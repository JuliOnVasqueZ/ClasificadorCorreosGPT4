Another concern was that the talks could jeopardize a legislative solution to Edison's problem. Lynch did notify her fellow commissioners, whose support she would need to pull off a settlement. PUC Commissioner Jeff Brown, one of three Davis appointees on the five-member board, said Lynch told him Sept. 21 "in the strictest confidence" about the negotiations with Edison. "If it became public, it could blow up," Brown said. "There would be too much hullabaloo."
