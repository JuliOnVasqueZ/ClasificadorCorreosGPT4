The gross margin of Power Producers in the spot market will be determined by the spark spread, and when these companies hedge, they will have to hedge using the forward spark spread available at that time. Thus, a declining spark spread affects the earnings power of all companies, even those who are well hedged. Only if they could hedge 100% would their earnings be unaffected by declining prices. No company we are aware of has been able to hedge 100% of their output. The forward spark spread actually suggests a worse commodity price
