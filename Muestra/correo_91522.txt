Publications are displayed in Portable Document Format (PDF). Most of the energy database applications are written in Microsoft Access. STAT-USA, a U.S. Department of Commerce information service for business, economic, and trade professionals, distributes EnergyInfoDisc, through a cooperative agreement with EIA.
