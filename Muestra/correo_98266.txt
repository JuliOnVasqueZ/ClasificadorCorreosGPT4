Power and Associates Reports/ Nationwide Decline in Customer=20 Satisfaction of Electric Utility Service Among Midsize Businesses Price and Value Performance Hit Hard Across Nation; Satisfaction in California Continues to Plummet AGOURA HILLS, Calif., April 19 /PRNewswire/ -- Midsize business customers, who are responsible for approximately fifteen percent of the nation's electricity consumption, are very critical of electricity prices, according to the J.D.
