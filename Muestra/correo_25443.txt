Head of the Strategic Policy Issues in the Bank's Directorate Monetary Policy. Prior to his appointment to the Harvard faculty in July 1995, Professor Pill worked in London as an economist in the Monetary and Exchange Rate Policy Group of the Bank of England's Economics Division (1990-92). He has also worked at the International Monetary Fund in Washington, DC (1994, 1995). Professor Pill's current research has two dimensions. On the one hand, he is investigating the formulation and conduct of monetary policy in advanced economies, with a focus on the implementation of the single monetary policy in the euro area.
