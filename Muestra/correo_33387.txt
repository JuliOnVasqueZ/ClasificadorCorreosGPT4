Signed? AB 1x permits the CPUC to enter into a rate agreement with DWR, but such an agreement is not required by the statute. Given the irrevocable nature of DWR's proposed Agreement, is it necessary for the CPUC to approve the agreement, or will some other, less binding agreement suffice? One alternate approach to the Agreement is SB 18xx (Burton), which provides a dedicated rate to pay for the bonds and specifies exactly what DWR may include as a part of its costs. SB 18xx passed the Senate 35-2 on July 20, 2001, and is pending at the Assembly desk.
