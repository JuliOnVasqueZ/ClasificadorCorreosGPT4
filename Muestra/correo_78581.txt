In addition, every counterparty of Enron has to clear a credit check before they can transact through EnronOnline. The Credit Risk Management group approves a counterparty for a specific credit limit and tenor limit which are embedded within the EnronOnline database. Every transaction is passed through an electronic credit check to assure that the credit limits and tenor limits are not violated prior to Enron accepting the counterparty's bid or offer via EnronOnline, such credit checks are done automatically and there is no human intervention.
