Enron Wind announces first UK off-shore wind farm On 5th April 2001, Enron Wind announced that it had been granted the right to initiate the planning process for its first UK offshore wind farm, located at Gunfleet Sands, 7km from Clacton-upon-Sea in Essex. Construction is expected to start towards the end of 2002, with the wind farm of 30 turbines being operational by Q4 2003. Enron
