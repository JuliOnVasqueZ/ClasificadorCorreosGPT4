Fuel cells will likely hit the large stationary market first, powering strip malls and factories, according to David Redstone, editor of the Hydrogen and Fuel Cell Investor newsletter. "We're talking about unprecedented efficiency," he says, noting that fuel cells approach 60 percent efficiency (that is, 60 percent of the potential energy in fuel entering the cell actually results in energy coming out).
