These federal cases indicated that the "motivating factor" standard was applicable only to "mixed motive cases" in which the plaintiff had direct evidence of discrimination. Also troubling for Texas employers is the Court's secondary holding regarding the plaintiff's evidence of age discrimination. The company terminated the plaintiff's employment for documented poor performance.
