But nobody takes that forecast seriously. The CEC's numbers are way off base. California's electricity demand could exceed supply by 5%, or 2,000 MW, on days with typical summer weather. That 2,000 MW shortfall will be made up by turning out the lights in 2 million homes, or a mix of homes, businesses and industry. A bad heat wave would get shortages several times that size.
