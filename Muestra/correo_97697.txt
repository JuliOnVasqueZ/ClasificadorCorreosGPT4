The next 200 years may be more stable than the last and Microsoft and Cisco may prove more enduring than plantations. But we can hardly be sure. While 5 per cent may be a reasonable assumption for the growth rate of dividends in the US economy as a whole, it is likely that well before 2200 most of these will come from companies not yet founded. Suppose we accept Glassman and Hassett's protests that their dividend growth expectations are conservative.
