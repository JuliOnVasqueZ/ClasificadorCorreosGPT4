Of the eight large business segments for which GE reports results, two -- GE Capital and NBC -- account for nearly 50% of GE's five-year profit growth. Sure, GE Capital has benefited from its parent's dirt-cheap cost of capital, but from an investor's point of view, these advantages may be more than offset by the drag of laggardly earnings growth in other GE businesses.
