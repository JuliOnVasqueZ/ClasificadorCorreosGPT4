Unbelievably, key data was unavailable to Staff, such coincident peak load data, system-wide snap shots for days when TLRs were called, and import/export data. This lack of data creates a market inefficiency, because neither market participants nor regulators can fully analyze market conditions in real time. As such, the market is risk adverse, eschewing long-term deals for short-term transactions. Staff also noted that because the Security Coordinators often work for the IOU, there is a mixed incentive to enforce reliability on the grid
