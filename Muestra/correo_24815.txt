News Bush leaning toward execs for military The Associated Press 03/04/2001 The Seattle Times Sunday A9 WASHINGTON--Three corporate executives are under consideration to lead the Air Force, Army and Navy, administration officials said yesterday. The three men have been interviewed by Defense Secretary Donald Rumsfeld, and the White House was expected to announce next week that it will send their names to the Senate for confirmation, The Washington Times reported yesterday, quoting unidentified sources.
