Under the ICE, once a deal has been struck both counterparties will receive an electronic deal ticket which will then go through those players' own risk management systems. But the exchange is eager to look at ways of introducing its own clearing system. "No one really knows how at the moment," Sprecher said. "There might be some hybrid between traditional exchange clearing and what now exists peer to peer."
