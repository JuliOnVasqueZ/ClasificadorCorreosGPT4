The Energy Information Administration (EIA) has released its latest quarterly CD-ROM, Energy InfoDisc, which is now a two (2) disc product. Energy InfoDisc is a comprehensive, high-tech product that is easy to use, yet robust enough for serious number crunching and analysis.
