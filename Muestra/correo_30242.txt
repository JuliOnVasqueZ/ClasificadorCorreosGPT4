The TP is a calculated price between the highest decremental bid and the lowest incremental bid. All incremental bids above the TP are adjusted down to the TP and all decremental bids below the TP are adjusted up to it. Though designed to resolve the price overlap, there are certain difficulties with the TP as it was implemented.
