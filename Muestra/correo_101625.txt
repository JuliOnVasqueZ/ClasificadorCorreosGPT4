The battle pits investor-owned utilities against large manufacturers that want cheap power and independent power companies, such as Enron Corp., that want to compete with utilities. For one House Commerce subcommittee markup last year, lobbying firms paid temp workers to wait in line outside the hearing room for two days so the firms would have seats at the markup.
