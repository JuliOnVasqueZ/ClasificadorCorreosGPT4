Thanks, SRS Announcing the Formation of One Corporate Equity Investing Unit To better develop and manage equity investment opportunities related to our core businesses, Enron has formed one corporate equity investment unit. This new unit, Enron Principal Investments, will combine the existing investment units of ENA, EBS and Enron Investment Partners. Additionally, the Enron Special Asset Group will also become part of Enron Principal Investments The strategy of Enron Principal Investments will be to work with all the business units of Enron to identify, execute, and manage equity investments, which leverage Enron's unique and proprietary knowledge.
