The Lodi project is designed for full operation as having a 12 Bcf working capacity, with 400 MMcf/d injection and 500 MMcf/d withdrawal capacities. It will be connected to Pacific Gas and Electric Co.'s backbone transmission system through a 35-mile pipeline consisting of three miles of 30-inch-diameter and 32 miles of 24-inch-diameter pipe. During the past year's delay in start-up, Western has "moved the pipeline around a bit, but not a whole lot," Fossum said.
