HOUSTON -(Dow Jones)- U.S. natural gas physical prices fell Tuesday as demand eased, except in the Northeast and Southeast areas of the country, traders said. Heavy snowstorms in the upper Northeast supported some pricing, as did cooler weather in Georgia, the Carolinas and the Florida panhandle. Some storage buying occurred in Texas "if they could make it work," a trader said. "It was a pretty uneventful day," a Gulf Coast trader said late Tuesday.
