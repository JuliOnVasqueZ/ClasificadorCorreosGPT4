SYSTEMS Two (2) complete simple-cycle (hot-side) SCR systems including reactors, catalyst, ammonia injection grids and systems, CEMS duct sections, vaporizers, storage tank and controls. Current design allows for up to 60% NOx removal with expansion space available for over 80% NOx removal. Originally designed for Frame 6B's but possibly adaptable to LM6000's, W251's or multiple smaller gas turbines. Ideal to apply to fast-track CA project to comply with 2001 waivers and subsequently adapt to meet 2002 emissions requirements. Owners would consider equity in appropriate project and could assist qualified partners with immediate site and fuel, as well as possible availability
