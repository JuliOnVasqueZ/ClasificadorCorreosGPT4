Basketball ticket holders who are not planning to use their tickets for home games can now send them to the Texas Exes who will distribute the tickets to students for free. No money will be refunded to the ticket holder. The ticket giveaway will be coordinated through the Texas Exes Student Chapter and tickets will be available on a first-come first-served basis. This project is a collaborative effort between the Texas Exes and UT Athletics to increase attendance at home basketball games. Please
