While the bill does not (and cannot legally) bar interstate pipelines from coming into the state, it has provisions which will prevent large customers from signing up with the interstates. The large customers have to commit to take firm service on a 5 year basis with exit fees. If they don't take this, they can only get interruptible service from the utilities.
