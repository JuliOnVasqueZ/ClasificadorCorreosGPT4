Moreover, Order 2000 does not compel any transmission owner to join an RTO, but provides only regulatory guidance and incentives for willing participants, as well as a veiled threat of further consequences for the hold-outs. As to the final part of the question ("why should we care?"), presumably, the development of a fully-functioning RTO network will promote both the efficiency and market transparency goals of the original FERC orders.
