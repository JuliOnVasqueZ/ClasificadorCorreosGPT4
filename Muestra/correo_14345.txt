Heralded by professional defensive coaches as a "guru" and a "defensive genius" prior to his arrival at LSU, the former Illinois head coach led the LSU defense to its worst two-year performance in the history of the school (1893). If you are interested in immortalizing the Tepper legacy at LSU for your scrapbook, copies of his book "Complete Linebacking" can still be obtained (at a discounted price no less) at most local book outlets.
