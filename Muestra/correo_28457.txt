Trade zones such as Nafta created new linkages between the industrial world and its offshore suppliers: exports to the US account for 25 per cent of Mexican GDP, for example. Asia, excluding Japan, has also become tightly linked to the US IT demand cycle; close to 40 per cent of the region's overall economic growth in 2000 can be accounted for by surging IT exports to the US.
