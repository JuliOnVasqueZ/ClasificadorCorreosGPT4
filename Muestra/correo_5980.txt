Four appropriately sized Regional Transmission Organizations ("RTOs") that encompass robust, competitive spot markets for energy and operating reserve products (including demand side management products) will provide an efficient and timely solution for the construction of adequate generation, the siting of appropriate transmission and efficient use of demand side management products to ensure that demand is met in real time. Spot markets reveal the cost of procuring (or not using) electricity based on actual supply and demand conditions and, thus, signal the need for new generation capacity.
