Pinnacle Index What is the Pinnacle Index. It is simply the put/call ratio at key overhigh benchmark levels. Typically, there should be two (2) calls for every one (1) put. So when this ratio reaches extremes levels, it can presage key turning points in the stock. The average Pinnacle Index, therefore, should read 2.0.
