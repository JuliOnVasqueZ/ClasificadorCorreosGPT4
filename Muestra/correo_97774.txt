Also on the embedded bandwagon is Pennsylvania company Neoware Systems, which just announced its NeoLinux embedded Linux distribution based on Red Hat's Linux distribution. The company is offering NeoLinux for small handheld devices but is focusing more on "information appliances" - that is, network devices dedicated to a single task such as firewalls, routers and security devices.
