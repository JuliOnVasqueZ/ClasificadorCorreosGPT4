Last year, when a black freshman applied to the University of Georgia's all-white Alpha Gamma Delta, most of the members were adamant that she be rejected. "If we had a black girl in our sorority, none of the fraternities would want to do anything with us," one was quoted as saying. That incident came to light because one sorority member, Ali Davis, filed a racial discrimination complaint. UGA temporarily suspended Alpha Gamma Delta during an investigation.
