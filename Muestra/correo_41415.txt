The order initiates a regional collaborative process to foster RTO formation. The Order also imposes filing requirements on the privately owned "public utilities" that are subject to FERC jurisdiction, and requires these private utilities to describe in their filings how they have attempted to accommodate the needs of transmission owning state/municipal, cooperative and federally owned systems.
