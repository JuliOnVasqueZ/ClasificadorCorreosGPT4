Another problem is cross-border power trading, where contradictory legislation essentially gives a monopoly to the Polish Power Grid, also known as PSE. Enron and others protest that PSE shouldn't compete with electricity-market participants who contract with it for transmission service. PSE intends to stay in the trading business, says Marek Zerka, PSE's vice president.
