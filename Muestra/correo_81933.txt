AB 995 (Wright) and SB 1194 (Sher), which extend public benefit charges for energy conservation, renewable energy development, and RD&D, passed the State Legislature by large margins. Recent indications are that the Governor will sign the bills, although interested companies should join IEP in sending letters urging him to do so. Power plant siting was front and center the last week of the session. As recently as Wednesday of this week, the bill was so bad for new power plant development that IEP opposed the expedited siting provisions.
