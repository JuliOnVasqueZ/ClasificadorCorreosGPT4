Wood also recognized that some "sticks" are needed to entice RTO participation. Therefore, Wood proposes to have all public utilities come in by December 15, 2001 with a declaration to join an RTO. If they do not make this declaration, these entities will have their market based rate authority revoked on a prospective basis.
