The shepherd bill would give people who watch sheep some of the basic working conditions that other workers have in state law. Those include a 10-minute rest break for every four hours of work, tools paid for by the employer and toilets, lights and water in housing. It also would put into state law a $1,050-a-month minimum wage for sheepherders. While legislators toiled, Tuesday's terrorist attacks were never far from their minds.
