By the time NASDAQ had maxed out in the recent bull market, many financial commentators were claiming that something structural had changed in the economy: new economics and the increasing value of intangible assets and brands, among other things, were supposed to have replaced more traditional factors in determining stock market valuations. Nice in theory, perhaps, but wrong in practice. McKinsey analysis shows that the traditional triumvirate of earnings growth, inflation, and interest rates continues to drive stock prices.
