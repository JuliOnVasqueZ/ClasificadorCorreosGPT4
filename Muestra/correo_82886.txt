He credits the long-term power contracts with helping to cool the spot market for electricity, where prices in recent weeks have dropped sharply. In June, the state paid an average price of $167 a megawatt hour for electricity. That was down from $243 in May, though still far above the $25-to-$27 range of two years ago.
