Unlike Houston, three of these four cities offering lesser subsidies provided other incentives. In Dallas and Los Angeles, they note, the government condemned whole blocks of land for private development around the arena. In Atlanta, Ted Turner was given carte blanche to develop public land. All of the markets that received a smaller subsidy also had both major league basketball and hockey teams, which could share the construction costs.
