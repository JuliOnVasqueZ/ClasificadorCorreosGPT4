Part of the fallout from California's electricity crisis is considerable concern-even opposition-in other states to restructuring the electricity industry. Although a few states have decided, based on the California experience, not to restructure, I believe that is the wrong outcome. Instead, the primary lessons other states should learn from California are that competitive markets can work; competition is not to blame for California's problems; and basic economic principles of supply, demand, and prices affect market outcomes.
