Thanks On March 14, 01, FERC issued an "Order removing obstacles to increased electric generation and gas supply in the West.." (Docket No. EL01-47). Most significantly, FERC authorizes industrials in WSCC to sell power to licensed power marketers (like EPMI and EES) effective 3/14/01 through 12/31/01. The industrial does not have to obtain its own power marketer license from FERC (subject to EPMI and EES making filings for the industrials). (This is similar to the authorization that EPMI and EES requested nationwide earlier this year
