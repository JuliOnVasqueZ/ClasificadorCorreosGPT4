Chairman Baxter To Remain Chief Strategy 10/18/2000 Dow Jones News Service Enron Corp. (ENE) named J. Clifford Baxter, the chief strategy officer, vice chairman. A company spokeswoman said Baxter will retain his role as chief strategy officer. (An item that ran at 4:08 p.m. EDT stated that Baxter will relinquish this role. A company spokesman has since said Baxter will remain chief strategy officer).
