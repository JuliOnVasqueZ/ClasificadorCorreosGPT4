``We're almost as good as Western Europe, and Western Europe is about twice as energy-efficient as the United States Among the 50 states, only Texas consumes more energy -- measured by its total use of electricity, natural gas and oil -- than California, the nation's most populous state. However, California ranks 47th in per-capita energy use -- well below No. 4 Texas, No. 20 Washington and No. 27 Oregon, according to the Energy Information Administration, which is part of the U.S. Energy Department. When looking specifically at per-capita electricity consumption, California ranks 49th, at 24.2 million BTUs, the agency said.
