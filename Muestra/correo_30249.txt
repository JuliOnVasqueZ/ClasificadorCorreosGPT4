For example, a Generating Unit with a 1MW/minute ramp rate can only increase or decrease its output by 10 MW within a 10-minute interval, regardless of the combination of Ancillary Services capacity and Supplemental Energy bids it has submitted Thus, its qualifying capacity would be 10 MW. Priority in allocating bids to the qualifying capacity will be based first on lowest bid price, and then on increasing qualities of service. Although only qualifying capacity will be considered in the TP calculation, all bids within the price overlap will be adjusted to the resulting TP. *
