Macomb County reported 1,020 dog bites last year, up 9.5 percent from a year earlier. The Oakland County Animal Shelter, which covers much of the county, said the number of bites there remained constant at about 800. Nationally, the Center for Disease Control and Prevention said 4.7 million dog bites were reported in 1997.
