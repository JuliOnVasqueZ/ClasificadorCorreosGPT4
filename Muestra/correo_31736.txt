One option being discussed is to have Atlanta Gas Light offer a regulated gas service alongside unregulated marketers. That would make Georgia more like other states that have deregulated. Other measures could include new standards for billing, customer service and meter reading; more authority for the PSC, including emergency power to regulate prices; reducing the sales tax for gas bills; and increasing competition by allowing nonprofit electric cooperatives to become gas marketers.
