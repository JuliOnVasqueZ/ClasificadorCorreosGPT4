That relationship shall be determined based on common law as illustrated in case law and currently under U.S. Internal Revenue Service Revenue Ruling 87-41. Accordingly, for purposes of applying Opinion 25, a grantee meets the definition of an employee if the grantor consistently represents that individual to be an employee under common law. The definition of an employee for payroll tax purposes under the U.S. Internal Revenue Code includes common law employees.
