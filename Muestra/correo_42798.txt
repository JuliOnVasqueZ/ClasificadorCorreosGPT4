APGA Believes Market Manipulated=20 Unsatisfied by the federal government's explanation that the high gas prices last winter were due mainly to supply and demand, the American Public Gas Association says it believes the recent price drop is a signal that the market may have been unfairly manipulated. In a letter sent to the EIA and to key lawmakers, APGA insists that the supply/demand explanations do not tell the full story. Rather, the association of municipal gas systems believes price speculation played a major role in the dramatic upswing in prices during the 2000-2001 heating season.
