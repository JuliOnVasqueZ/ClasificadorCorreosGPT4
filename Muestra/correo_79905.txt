Yesterday, the Supreme Court issued an order instructing the parties to file, by November 30, additional briefs on the qui tam relator's standing to bring an FCA suit. A panel of the Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals recently issued an opinion declaring qui tam enforcement unconstitutional, a ruling that would have created a conflict among the circuits.
