Florida law prohibits companies from building power plants unless virtually all of the electricity is for use in Florida. Merchant power companies, however, make their money by selling power on the wholesale market to other power suppliers. Michael Green, Duke Energy vice president and PACE chairman, said a deregulated Florida wholesale market will lead to lower costs for consumers and more dependable energy supplies. Investor-owned utilities such as FPL Group and Florida Power Corp. counter that they make plenty of energy, and prices are already among the nation's lowest.
