To this end, the Commission proposes that each public utility under the FPA (public utility) would no longer file: short-term or long-term service agreements for market-based sales of electric energy; service agreements for those generally applicable services, such as point-to-point transmission service, for which the public utility has a standard form of agreement under its tariff; and Quarterly Transaction Reports summarizing its short-term sales and purchases of power at market-based rates. In lieu of the above listed filings, each public utility would file electronically with the Commission and post on its website an Index of Customers
