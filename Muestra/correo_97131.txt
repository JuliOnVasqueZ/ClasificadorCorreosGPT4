In previous gas price spikes or periods of low oil prices, processors have elected to leave ethane in the gas stream, providing an incremental source of gas supply. The observed difference in ethane production levels in recent years demonstrates a flexibility to add approximately 600 MMcf per day of gas supply, should extracting ethane from the gas stream become clearly unprofitable.
