Separation Of Producers, Grids The Key Even if Germany eventually establishes a reference price, the problem of vertical integration remains. One needs unbundling (between producers and grids) to open the way for competition, ABB's Kollberg said. According to ABB's own analysis, between 78% and 83% of German power transmission lines are owned and controlled by the big German power producers.
