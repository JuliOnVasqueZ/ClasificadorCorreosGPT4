Only two years ago, Sithe Energies was one of the largest independent power producers in the United States, operating 43 power plants from New Jersey to California. Sithe Energies had made a decided effort to purchase vast generation assets in the Northeastern United States, believing that there would be value in having critical mass in the deregulated generation market in that region. Until last year, Sithe Energies was the primary subsidiary of French mega-utility Vivendi, which had been creating a reputation as an industry powerhouse
