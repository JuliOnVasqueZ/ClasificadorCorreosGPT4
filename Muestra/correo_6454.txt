Of particular note is page 40, lines 21-23, which says that the form, structure and operating entity of the RTO must be approved by the participating utilities. At a minimum, this language gives the utilities a veto over other market participants. This could also be read as saying the utilities and only the transmitting utilities make these critical governance decisions without participation by others.
