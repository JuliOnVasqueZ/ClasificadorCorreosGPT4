There is no specific mention of authority to enter into transactions (by Merced's traders). Intuitively one could draw the conclusion that the ISDA Master contemplates transactions, Merced authorized the ISDA Master and therefore Merced authorized transactions under the ISDA Master. Since Merced is a governmental entity, the safer view is that we should not assume that Merced authorized transactions. Merced probably intended to authorize transactions, and if so, should not have an issue with specifically providing a clarifying resolution Sara
